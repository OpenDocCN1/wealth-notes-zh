# Who owns Sears ?

Sears, Roebuck and Co. has over 158,000 share owners.
By far the largest of them—with holdings of more than $1,500,000,000 in Sears stock—is the Sears Employees Profit Sharing Fund. This personal stake in the company is one reason why Sears people serve you so well.

Today, more than one quarter of all Sears stock—almost 20,000,000 shares—is owned by the Sears Employees Savings and Profit Sharing Pension Fund.
More than 90 percent of eligible Sears employees (one year's service) are members of this Fund. The benefits they draw from the Fund on retirement give them an unusual degree of financial security.
Profit sharing has given Sears people something more than pride in their job: pride of ownership. The feeling of extra responsibility that comes from having a financial stake

in their company's success.
This feeling shows itself in the devoted skill of Sears buyers. In the friendly helpfulness of Sears salespeople. In the speed and accuracy of Sears mail-order personnel. In the scrupulous care of the scientists in the Sears testing laboratory.
Of course, self-interest is by no means the only reason why Sears people serve you so cheerfully and well. But it does give them a powerful incentive.
They know that—by satisfying you—they keep feathering their nest and fostering their nest egg.
