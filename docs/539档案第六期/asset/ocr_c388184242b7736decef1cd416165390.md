# The case of the "INVISIBLE SANDWICH"

BETWEEN you and danger on the highway, there rides an "invisible sandwich" of safety. It's your windshield which is made of a tough, transparent sheet of plastic that bonds two pieces of glass together. It's the only kind of safety glass that passes the American Standards Association specifications for American car windshields. This plastic-glass sandwich is Laminated Safety Glass, and today most cars have LSG also in side windows.
The plastic interlayer in Laminated Safety Glass reduces the hazard of flying glass. A rock striking this "sandwich" may crack the glass, but the plastic layer holds the glass in a tight grip to keep it from breaking into dangerous fragments.
LSG won't "trap" you! Because of its unique ability to "give", you can quickly kick or knock LSG out of a window frame in case doors and windows are jammed shut.

![](bbox=[0.51, 0.5, 0.597, 0.56])

Your windshield is always Laminated Safety Glass. Your side windows also are LSG if you see a distinct "line" on an exposed edge. That "line" is actually the edge of the plastic present only in Laminated Safety Glass. If you don't find LSG in your present car, be sure it's in your next one.
And if any of the safety glass in your car is scratched, broken or chipped, see your local auto-glass dealer about replacement. The price of safe vision is very low; the cost of poor vision, incalculable!
