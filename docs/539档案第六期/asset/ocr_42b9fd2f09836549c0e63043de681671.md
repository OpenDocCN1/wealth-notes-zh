The Lennox reputation for dependability evidently boomerangs on us in a few instances. This results (we think) from the unconscious comparison people make with other so-called luxury lines. A luxurious automobile costs more than many people can afford. Why would not a heating and/or cooling unit with a reputation like Lennox cost more?

Because the so-called luxury features of a Lennox unit are designed to hold operating and maintenance costs down almost as much as they are designed to provide comfort. They save fuel. They are necessities.

You are going to spend several times as much to operate your heating-cooling unit as you are to buy it. First costs are important—but operating costs are what eat up budgets.
