## 6、为什么没人能否定西尔斯实验室？
### Why nobody at Sears can overrule this laboratory

Sears, Roebuck and Co. helps give you better value for your money by maintaining the world’s largest private laboratory—and staff of graduate scientists—for testing general merchandise. It is here that Sears checks quality; improves old products; develops new ones.

SEARS welcomes customers who ask a lot of questions about the products they consider buying.
But few of their questions are half as searching as the ones Sears asks its own testing laboratory—before Sears items go on sale.
Is this fabric really fadeproof and shrinkproof? How long will this new record changer run without being serviced? Which dishwasher, "A" or "B," is more efficient?

The laboratory answers thousands of such questions every year. It presents the impartial facts, based on scientific testing.
More than two-thirds of its staff are graduate chemists, physicists or engineers. The information they provide constantly leads to product improvement—which means better and better value for your money.

What’s more, the laboratory has absolute veto power over any product that does not measure up to the company’s high standards. This makes it virtually impossible for shoddy goods to find their way into Sears stores or the Sears catalog.
Since it was started in 1911, this laboratory has cost some $50,000,000. But no Sears investment has paid bigger dividends—in the form of satisfied customers.
