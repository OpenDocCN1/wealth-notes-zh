
## 贝尔先生的发明后来怎样了？

80 年前，亚历山大·格雷厄姆·贝尔发明了电话。这个彻底改变了人与人之间的沟通的简单仪器只有 12 个部分。西电公司今天制造的贝尔电话——尽管看起来很简单——却有将近 500 个部分。

但如果电话的部件增加了 40 倍，那么它的方便性和实用性也应该成倍增长，直到我们给大陆另一端，大洋另一端的人打电话，也能听得清清楚楚。

今天的电话，有些加入了提高音量的按钮，让听者听得更清楚。有些加入了切换通话的按钮。还有些按键带背光，这样在黑暗的地方，拨号也很方便。有些加入了免提功能，不拿话筒就可以轻松拨打电话。还有些有自动答录电话的功能。

这些便利的现代功能，都是西电人制造的，他们不断地改进 1882 年的贝尔系统。这些年，我们的电话制造已经取得了巨大进步。我们现在的主要工作是，把实验室的想法转化为高效、经济的可批量生产产品。

在贝尔中央办公室幕后，你可以看到我们的成就。以前电话切换全部要靠手动，而现在，当你手指转动刻度盘时，一系列自动转换设备就会做出相应的反应。甚至还有更快，更好的交换设备。

今天，由西电公司制造的设备，一条铜线能一次承担 1800 个通话。无线电中继传输系统能加速全国电视和通话信号传输。

那么，更好的电话通讯能发展到什么程度呢？

一个有趣的预测是这样的：有一天，每个人出生时就会有一个电话号码，并佩戴一个手表大小带有按钮的仪器，可以在任何地方给任何人打电话。

人们也觉得不可思议。可是，不管未来的电话形状如何改变，西电公司——作为贝尔系统制造商都会不断完善它的功能——会不断根据需求完善机器和系统——把想法巧妙，经济地变成产品，让贝尔电话服务变得更实用和更方便。

IT'S not too easy to be a gloom-chaser after a full day's work at a regular job. Yet it's something Elaine Gereg of our Allentown, Pa. plant has been doing for a long time as a Gray Lady of the American Red Cross.

## Cancer... like a small spreading flame... demands prompt action

At the first sign of trouble—and cancer often causes early warning symptoms—see your physician right away.

In doing so, the chances of cure are greatly increased. In fact, it is estimated that if all cancer patients were treated early and adequately, one half could be saved.

Prompt action, then, is your most important personal safeguard against cancer. Fortunately, medical science offers others.

One notable example is a simple test—the so-called "Pap Smear"—by which one common form of cancer in women can be detected early, or even before symptoms appear. If every adult woman took this test every year, the type of cancer that it reveals could almost be eliminated as a cause of death.

Moreover, your physician can often detect other forms of cancer—in both men and women—while the disease is still localized. At this early stage, treatment by surgery, X-ray and other forms of radiation is much more likely to be successful.

Since early detection is an important factor in the outcome of cancer, all adults should have periodic health examinations and, between checkups, watch for these seven warnings of cancer:

- 1. Unusual bleeding or discharge.
- 2. A lump or thickening in the breast or elsewhere.
- 3. A sore that does not heal.
- 4. Change in bowel or bladder habits.
- 5. Hoarseness or cough.
- 6. Indigestion or difficulty in swallowing.
- 7. Change in a wart or mole.

These symptoms, though often caused by some condition other than cancer, should never be ignored. Should any one of them last longer than two weeks, don't neglect to see your physician immediately.
