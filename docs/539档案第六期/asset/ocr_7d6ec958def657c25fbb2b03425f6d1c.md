BATTLE AGAINST WEATHER

> Story of the struggle to develop a house paint that will last years longer—under all weather conditions in the U.S.A.!

NEAR SAYVILLE, N. Y., is a bleak and quiet meadow. Standing in its center are rows and rows of painted, fence-like structures, nothing more.

But in this quiet meadow a bitter contest has been fought—for over forty-two years. It has its victories and defeats. Few of them would merit a line in your local newspaper. Yet every homeowner who cares about maintaining his home has a stake in what happens in Sayville.

The opponents in the contest are paint and the elements. The elements seem to be favored, for the paint on the test "fences" is scarcely 2/1000ths of an inch thick, while the weather goes from freezing rain and frost in winter to baking heat in summer. Changes in temperature and humidity are frequent—and sudden.

But while the weather does its worst, science does its best—in this case, science in the service of National Lead Company, makers of "Dutch Boy" House Paint. What can weather do to house paint? Why does it fade, blister, chalk, peel? What about moisture, or dryness, or heat, or icy cold? How long can a paint job stand the weather? Answers to these questions must come from the "fences," where over 25,000 "tests" are on exposure every day. As the answers come, back they go to the "Dutch Boy" laboratories. Soon new paints come out to be brushed on the "fences," and the battle begins again.

The major victory at Sayville—the victory of greatest significance to homeowners—has been the development of "Dutch Boy" House Paint. All the years of testing, all

the hours in the laboratory, have had this result: the formulation of a paint homeowners now call "the 5-year house paint." "Five years," remember, is what they say—in a country where painting a house every three or four years is common!

Year after year, over thousands of tests, "Dutch Boy" House Paint has been brought to a near-perfect balance of beauty and durability. The tests show that it keeps its color better, resists blistering, mildew, metal staining, shows greater freedom from cracking and scaling, than all the other paints tested with it.
