# Quicker Way to More Turkey Flavor... and More Meat

It's now as traditional as cranberry jelly ... Reynolds Wrap to roast your turkey in.

And how much better it is! Wrapped in this pure aluminum foil, your turkey can be cooked at higher heat, in much less time. And you don't have to watch it. No basting. You can relax with your guests. No oven splattering, either—the juices are sealed in. That's why you get more flavor and actually more meat when you roast in Reynolds Wrap.

Here's the tested Reynolds Wrap higher-heat method (more details in new booklet banded to packages). Lay trussed turkey, breast up, on long sheet of Reynolds Wrap (use 18" wide Heavy Duty or join two 12" widths together by double-folding edges). Put small folds of foil over wing tips and ends of legs to prevent puncturing. Now bring foil up, overlapping edges 3" and molding to shape of bird. Place in shallow pan in a hot (450° ) oven, preheated. Following are approximate roasting times:

+   (Ready-to-cook weight before stuffing)
6-9 lbs.... 2½ to 2½ hrs.
10-13 lbs.... 2¾ to 3 hrs.
14-17 lbs.... 3 to 3½ hrs.
18-21 lbs.... 3½ to 3½ hrs.
22-24 lbs.... 3½ to 3¾ hrs.

Now open the foil and admire your plump, golden turkey. (Meat thermometer inserted in thigh should read about 190°.) If you want it browner, roast 15 minutes more—foil open. Make gravy your usual way—and bow to the praise!
