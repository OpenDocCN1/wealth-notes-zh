# 18、关于新经济型汽车你应该知道的事

# Some Things
You Should Know About the
New Economy Cars

IF YOU THINK Detroit's new compact cars are all of a kind, you're in for a large surprise.

For instance, only one of Detroit's new compact cars was proved over every mile of U.S. numbered highway before its public introduction.

Only one of Detroit's three new compact cars gives honest-to-goodness, 6-passenger comfort.

Only one of these new compact cars is priced up to $124 less than other 6-passenger compact cars in its field.*

This economy car is the Ford Falcon, the New-size Ford that gives you more than twice as much trunk space as rear-engine cars ... nearly 24 cubic feet... room for all 6 passengers' luggage. And the Falcon gives you full 6-passenger room. There's no gearshift coming up out of the floor to crowd and annoy the middle front-seat passenger. Instead of being priced the same as other cars in its field, though, a Falcon is priced lower—up to $124 lower than other comparable 6-passenger models.* Falcon is America's lowest priced 6-passenger car.

Falcon savings only begin with a lower price. You get up to 30 miles per gallon with regular gas... aluminized mufflers normally last twice as long as the ordinary kind... important items such as arm rests, sun visors and coat hooks are standard... you don't need power brakes or power steering with the easy-handling Falcon... and thanks to lower-cost servicing, you can enjoy insurance savings up to 15%.

Here's another thing you may not know about the economy-car market. In addition to a compact-economy size, Ford offers a large economy size... the Fairlane 500. This value leader of the industry has increased shoulder, hip and leg room generously. It's designed for those who want savings like the Falcon's, but in luxury-car size, and with the Thunderbird's kind of styling, power and performance. And yet this Fairlane 500 is priced $142 less than last year.*

With the Falcon and the Fairlane 500, Ford brings you two new low-price fields... the economy and value choice of a lifetime.

*Price comparisons are based upon manufacturers' suggested retail delivered prices.
