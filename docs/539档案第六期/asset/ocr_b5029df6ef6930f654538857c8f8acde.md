# Where do great ideas come from?

From its beginnings, this nation has been guided by great ideas.

The men who hammered out the Constitution and the Bill of Rights were thinkers—the educated men of their day.

And the advances in our civilization since that time have come from minds equipped by education to create and put great ideas into action.

It is in the college classroom that the great ideas of the future will be born.

That is why the present tasks of our colleges and universities are of vi-

cal concern to every American. These institutions are doing their utmost to meet the steadily rising pressure for enrollment and provide the healthy educational climate in which great ideas may flourish.

But they need help, and they need it now!
