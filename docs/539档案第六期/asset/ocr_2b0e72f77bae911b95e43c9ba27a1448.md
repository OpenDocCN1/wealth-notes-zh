# 5、被困在家中了？你只要打个电话，开个账户，就能在西尔斯下单

# Trapped at home? It takes
one call to open a charge account
and place an order at Sears

At Sears, Roebuck and Co., you can charge any item Sears sells. And you can do it by telephone. There's no down payment needed. Read why 12,198,987 thrifty shoppers now have active credit accounts at Sears.

Simply call Sears to open an account. From that moment on, you can order anything Sears has to offer—by telephone. Satisfaction guaranteed or your money back.

Which plan is best for you?

1. Sears Revolving Charge. This plan gives you complete charge privileges on any merchandise in the Sears catalog, and at any Sears store. No minimum purchase necessary.

When you receive your monthly statement, you can pay the entire amount with no service charge. Or you can make monthly payments and spread the balance over several months with a small service charge.

2. Sears Easy Payment Plan. Depending on the amount you want to charge, you have up to 18 or 24 months to pay—even up to 36 months on home appliances. The initial purchase must be at least $20, but after that you can add purchases as low as $5 to your balance.

3. Sears Modernizing Credit Plan. This plan is for Home Improvement purchases. You can open an account for only $100, and add smaller purchases to your balance as you go along. The time allowed you for payment can be as much as five years—depending on how much you buy.

The Sears number is in your phone directory and in the Sears catalog.

Why not do your Christmas shopping at Sears by phone? Sears sales operators will look through the catalog for you, describe merchandise, tell you about special bargains.
