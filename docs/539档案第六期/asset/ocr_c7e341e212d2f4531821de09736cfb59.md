# Do You Own Your Car...
Or Does Your Car Own You?

by GEORGE ROMNEY
President
American Motors Corporation

If you are losing the battle of the budget, the culprit may be sitting in your driveway.

For the average American family spends 10% of income after taxes to keep the big family "bus" going, according to surveys. Some actually spend a full half of income for the questionable privilege of driving a big, chrome-plated, gas-hungry car. Overweight, expensive cars are keeping millions of families "broke," because the car owns the family, instead of the family owning the car.

# Look at your car budget

Have you totaled up the expense of running your car? Many motorists are shocked to discover that their big cars are costing them as much as $1,500 a year, counting gasoline, oil, depreciation and other incidentals.
