How an Ohio mother taught her sons an appreciation of fine foods that inspired a fabulous business

At the turn of the century, in the midst of country plenty, no table in all Ohio held more delight than Mahala Stouffer's. Mahala chose the finest of the rich abundance of American foods. Mealtime seemed all tempting tastes and blissful fragrances. Mahala's standards of good taste developed in her sons an appreciation of fine foods. From this came their interest in starting a restaurant dedicated to serving dishes even Mahala would praise. Anything so good will prosper. The restaurants multiplied. Today they number 26, and over 100,000 people daily in Cleveland, Detroit, New York, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh and Chicago dine on the same good dishes Mahala served. The compliments Mahala once got for her food are counted in millions every month, — millions of compliments to the skilled Stouffer women cooks. Now, many of these same compliment-winning dishes are being frozen for you to serve at home. Now, all of America can serve Stouffer's Frozen Cooked Foods — the new frozen foods made from favorite recipes of famous restaurants. Look for Stouffer's in your grocer's freezer when you shop.
