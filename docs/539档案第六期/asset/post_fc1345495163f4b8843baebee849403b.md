## 玻璃间的塑料层

## 解决了“隐形三明治”的问题

高速公路上，在你和危险之间，有一层安全的“隐形三明治”。它就是用结实，透明的塑料板材把两块玻璃紧紧粘在一起的挡风玻璃。这是唯一一种通过了美国标准协会对汽车挡风玻璃的要求的安全玻璃。这种塑料-玻璃三明治是夹层安全玻璃，如今大多数汽车侧窗都安装有夹层安全玻璃。

夹层安全玻璃大大降低了碎玻璃带来的危害。当有岩石撞击这种“三明治”时，玻璃可能出现裂纹，但是里面的塑料层会牢牢抓住玻璃产生的碎片。

夹层安全玻璃不会“难为”你！因为它有独特的“给予”能力，如果门窗卡住了，无法打开，你可以把夹层安全玻璃从门框或窗框中快速地敲或推出来。

你的挡风玻璃一般都是夹层安全玻璃。如果你侧面玻璃的裸露边缘看到了明显的“线”，说明它也是夹层安全玻璃。这条“线”，实际上就是夹层安全玻璃里面的塑料边缘。如果你发现现在车上安装的不是夹层安全玻璃，那么购买下一辆车时，一定要选带有夹层安全玻璃的。

如果你车上的安全玻璃被划伤，损坏，有碎裂处，请联系当地汽车玻璃经销商进行更换。花很少的钱，你就能得到安全；但是，如果不花这个钱，一旦发生危险，那损失将是无法估量的。

### Does your family's car have the "Line of Safety"?

Next time you're in your car, examine any exposed edge of glass in a window or door vent. Is there a distinctive line visible in the edge of the glass? If there is, be very glad it's there.

The line you see is actually a tough, completely transparent sheet of plastic bonded between two pieces of glass. The window is literally a "plastic-glass sandwich." It is Laminated Safety Glass.

You can't see the edge of your windshield, but you don't need to. Laminated Safety Glass is used in the windshield of every car made in the United States. Most cars provide Laminated Safety Glass in side and door windows as well. Here's how Laminated Safety Glass helps protect you:

![](img/8cffddf1c8314e0aa1e8e826db1d033a_104_1.png)

Suppose a rock was hurled against your car. If it struck your windshield—or a side window made of Laminated Safety Glass—the glass might break. But the tough plastic interlayer would tend to hold the glass in a tight grip and keep the broken pieces from flying apart, reducing the hazard of shattering glass. Other types of safety glass do not have this plastic interlayer.

![](img/8cffddf1c8314e0aa1e8e826db1d033a_104_2.png)

The plastic interlayer also acts as a resilient cushion that reduces the shock of impact. Another advantage of this unique ability to "give" is that it allows Laminated Safety Glass to be knocked or kicked out of a window frame. You have Laminated Safety Glass in your windshield. Check to see if the "Line of Safety" is in your side windows as well.
