**分享**

回到正题讲 dropshipping。

主要分**为3大板块：选品、推广、运营**。

刚起步时我们人少，所以挑核心工作去做，也就是**决定业务成败最关键的部分：选品和推广**。其实每一个板块拆分出来其实有很多工作可以做。要成功通过 facebook 广告推出一个爆款出来，我们最后总结出来：**选品占 40%，广告素材 35%，受众定位 25%**。爆款当中其实也会有分大小，我们暂借归类为：小爆，中爆，大爆，颠覆人生爆。各自对应 100-200美金/天，300-400美金/天，1k-2k美金/天，3k以上/天，可以做个参考。

**选品**

接下来说下选品。

dropshipping的选品是个频率很高的工作。我们一周大概要测试30-40个产品，多的时候一周60-70个都有，看自己情况和预算。每个产品我们会给个100刀的预算，能测试跑出来的，我们才会考虑扩量，否则直接 pass 下一个。

我们自己的情况是平均一周出一个爆款，中爆级别的。我也问过很多同行，我们的数据还是比较好的。所以可以平均下，50个左右能测试出一个款出来。

**这里面如果提高测试成功率呢？**

那么我们要做好选品工作。刚才我说了，选品占的因素会有40%。

**选品的思路**

刚需，受众广，最好是针对女性。建议选可以卖24.99美金以上+有现成视频素材（这个待会说为什么）另外还有个捷径是直接寻找已经热卖的款（跟别人的款），这个对新手来说成功率会更高些，缺点是广告竞价竞争激烈。

**选品方式**

有很多包含但不包括，比如 facebook刷购物广告，平台会推给你更多的购物广告。订阅竞争对手的网站，看亚马逊迹、ebay的商品排名，海外各大产品博客网站，YTB/IG 网红频道。

## TARGET GRAPH

SPECIFIC INTEREST GROUPS FOR TARGETING

- Federations, Associations, Confederations, Unions, Groups, Societies
- Websites, Forums
- Events, Meetings, Seminars, Ceremonies, Parties, Exhibitions
- Magazines, Journals, Newspapers
- Celebrities, Stars, Public Figures
- Job Titles
- Products, Equipments
- Brands, Companies
- Championships, Leagues, Tournaments
- Types, Categories, Forms, Styles, Models, Classes
- TV Show, Film, Radio, Online channel
- Broad
- Special Days (Anniversary, Birthday, Wedding, Holiday, Father's/Mom's day...)
- Fan Pages

![](img/5688f8284c7103ed77046cfddaf3da13_135_0.png)

Target Graph will help you to find potential audiences to sell your products. There are 14 interest groups in which you can target a specific audience and test the products you want.

You can choose one or more groups depending on your budget and start your campaign(s). If your product is suitable for all groups and you have enough budget, then you can test the product in each group and find multiple audiences who are interested in your product.

Example: You have a budget of $30/day and you want to test a yoga mat. You can target that you think can become a winning product. In this case, the majority of people will start standard campaigns with typical interests like - "Yoga", "Yoga Mat", "Yoga Teacher", "Yoga Instructor", "Yogaworks" and spend all the budget on this type of ad(s). In most cases, this method can be inefficient, and the target audience may not include potential buyers and of course, this will let you lose more money.

With the help of Target Graph, you can reach people who are most likely to become your customer. You can spend $30/day in a more effective way by choosing interest groups for your campaign. Let's imagine that you will use 3 groups: Magazines, Brands and Types (Categories). In this case, you need to create 1 campaign with 3 different ad sets targeting different interest groups with $10/day budget (as shown below).

- **AD SET #1 ($10/day)**
  Magazines, Journals, Newspapers
  - Elephant journal
  - OM Yoga & Lifestyle Magazine
  - Spirituality & Health Magazine
  - Yoga Journal
  - Yoga Magazine
  - YogaDork

- **AD SET #2 ($10/day)**
  Brands, Companies
  - Gaiam
  - Manduka
  - Onzie Yoga Apparel
  - Prana
  - Sweaty Betty

- **AD SET #3 ($10/day)**
  Types, Categories, Forms, Styles...
  - Bikram Yoga
  - Hatha yoga
  - Hot yoga
  - Iyengar Yoga
  - Kundalini yoga
  - Yin yoga

Totally you will be spending $30/day but your campaign will cover different specific interest groups. This method helps you to find where are you most potential customers.
