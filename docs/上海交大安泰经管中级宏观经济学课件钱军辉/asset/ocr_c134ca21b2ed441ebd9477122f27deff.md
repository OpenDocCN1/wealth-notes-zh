# 人口增长率和人均收入

![](bbox=[0.137, 0.216, 0.879, 0.746])

**International Evidence on Population Growth and Income per Person** This figure is a scatterplot of data from 96 countries. It shows that countries with high rates of population growth tend to have low levels of income per person, as the Solow model predicts.

*Source:* Alan Heston, Robert Summers, and Bettina Aten, Penn World Table Version 6.2, Center for International Comparisons of Production, Income and Prices at the University of Pennsylvania, September 2006.
