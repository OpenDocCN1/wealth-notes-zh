# Whole-Wheat Bubbles

You will find that package filled with bubbles—airy, thin and tasty—puffed from grains of wheat.
The grains are roasted by a fleeting heat until they taste like roasted nuts. They are puffed to eight times normal size by internal steam explosions. They are porous and fragile and crisp.
They are sized for digestion as grains never were before. Prof. Anderson’s process—chopping them grain—has liberated every granule to please. Every element and atom is made available as food.
Millions enjoy Puffed Grains morning, noon and night. They serve with cream and sugar—clear rice, those with berries—they float them in bowls of milk. And countless children when as play carry Puffed Grains with them.
This offer is made to let other millions know them. We want your folks included
