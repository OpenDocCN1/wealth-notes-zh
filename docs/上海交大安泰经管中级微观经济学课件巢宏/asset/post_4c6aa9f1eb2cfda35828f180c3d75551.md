## The Data

+   - 158 unique bets on a wheel
+     Single number, two numbers, three numbers, six numbers, column (not used), even money (not used)
+   - What’s near?
+     One square away on table
+     One slot away on wheel
+   - DV: In round t, did gambler place a bet on a near miss number in round t-1
+   - Controls: previous bets (favorites), number of existing near miss bets (mechanical)

## The Setting

+   - Rapid Roulette: individual touch-screen
+     Real wheel with croupier in center
+     30 seconds placing bets, 30 seconds spinning the wheel
+     Large casino in Reno/Tahoe, Saturday night 9pm-1am
+     401 unique spins, 36 unique players*, 6390 bets placed

*cash balance to 0 new buy-in within 5 minutes = same player, outside 5 minutes = new player
