# Marginal Revenue and Own-Price Elasticity of Demand

MR(q) = p(q)\left[1 + \frac{1}{\varepsilon}\right] says that the rate at which a seller’s revenue changes with the number of units it sells depends on the sensitivity of quantity demanded to price; i.e., upon the own-price elasticity of demand.
