# $ Equivalent Utility Gains

+   - Suppose gasoline can be bought only in lumps of one gallon.
+   - Use r₁ to denote the most a single consumer would pay for a 1st gallon -- call this her reservation price for the 1st gallon.
+   - r₁ is the dollar equivalent of the marginal utility of the 1st gallon.
