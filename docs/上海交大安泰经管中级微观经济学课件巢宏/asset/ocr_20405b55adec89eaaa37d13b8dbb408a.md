# Why Use a Reserve Price?

+   - I.e. with chance 1/4 each, the seller believes she faces buyer valuations ($20,$20), ($20,$50), ($50,$20) and ($50,$50).
+   - Use an English auction.
+   - Bids must be raised by at least $1.
+   - With chance 1/4 each, winning bids will be $20, $21, $21 and $50 if there is no reserve price.
