
## The Strong Axiom of Revealed Preference

- ◆ If the bundle **x** is revealed (directly or indirectly) as preferred to the bundle **y** and **x ≠ y**, then it is never the case that the **y** is revealed (directly or indirectly) as preferred to **x**; *i.e.*
- 
- **x ≽ᴰ y** or **x ≽ᴵ y**
- 
- ➜ not (**y ≽ᴰ x** or **y ≽ᴵ x**).
