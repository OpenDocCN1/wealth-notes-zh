- ♦ Yes, it is the same calculation.

q = D(p) = a − b p ⇔ p = (a − q)/b = D⁻¹(q),

the equation of the inverse market demand curve. And

q = S(p) = c + d p ⇔ p = (−c + q)/d = S⁻¹(q),

the equation of the inverse market supply curve.
