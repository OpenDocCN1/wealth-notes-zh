## Producer’s Surplus Revisited

So the firm’s producer’s surplus is

$$PS(p)=\int_{0}^{y^*(p)}[p-MC_s(z)]\,d(z)$$

$$=py^*(p)-\int_{0}^{y^*(p)}MC_s(z)\,d(z)$$

$$=py^*(p)-c_v(y^*(p)).$$

That is, **PS = Revenue − Variable Cost**

## Producer’s Surplus Revisited

- ♦ PS = Revenue - Variable Cost.
- ♦ Profit = Revenue - Total Cost
  = Revenue - Fixed Cost
    - Variable Cost.
- ♦ So, PS = Profit + Fixed Cost.
- ♦ Only if fixed cost is zero (the long-run) are PS and profit the same.

## Short-Run Industry Equilibrium

- - In a short-run, neither entry nor exit can occur.
- - Consequently, in a short-run equilibrium, some firms may earn positive economics profits, others may suffer economic losses, and still others may earn zero economic profit.
