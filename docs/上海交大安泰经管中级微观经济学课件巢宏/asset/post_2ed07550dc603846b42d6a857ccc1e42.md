## Inside bets:

- A  straight up (36 for 1; 35 to 1)
- B  line bet (18 for 1; 17 to 1)
- C  row bet (12 for 1; 11 to 1)
- D  corner bet (9 for 1; 8 to 1)
- E  six-bet (6 for 1; 5 to 1)

## Outside bets:

- F, G, H  even-money bets (red/black, even/odd, high/low)
- I          2 to 1 bets (first 12, second 12, third 12)
- All lose if 0 or 00 appears (alternative rules)

Almost all bets house edge 5.26% (5-way 7.89%, rarely observed)

## GF vs. HO: Inside Bets--Aggregate

### Logit regression

$P_{ijt}=1$ if person $i$ placed a bet on number $j$ on spin $t$, 0 otherwise

$H_{ijt}$ = how many times has number $j$ appeared previous to spin $t$ but while person $i$ was at the table minus its expected frequency of appearance $(1-(37/38)^{t-1})$

$F_{ijt}$ = favorite numbers, % of spins player $i$ has bet on number $j$ previous to period $t$

$L_{ijt}$ = winning chip left on table

**Conclusions:** Aggregate evidence for HO but lots of individual heterogeneity

## Reconciling the Results

Why HO in aggregate but even split of GF and HO at individual level?

### Possibility 1: HO gamblers bet more

HO gamblers stay longer (35.7 spins vs 34.1 spins)

...but bet less per spin (11.0 numbers vs 14.2 numbers)

### Possibility 2: HO gamblers are more consistent in their play

55% of GF players are non-significant, only 52% of HO players are

| Average coefficient on hotness | GF | HO |
|---|---|---|
| All 112 | -4.01 | 4.28 |
| Significant 36 | -5.51 | 6.53 |
| All 39 | -1.38 | 2.06 |
| Significant 18 | -2.31 | 3.54 |

## HH vs. SL: Inside Bets--Aggregate

Increase bets when winning, decrease when losing (HH) or decrease bets when winning, increase bets when losing (SL)

### Two measures of bet changes

- Quitting: 80% quit after loss, 20% after a win (baseline 28% wins)
- Less likely to quit after a win, HH

Average numbers bet after win/loss

|  | Avg. | Std. | N |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Spin | 7.63 | 6.12 | 139 |
| Won Prior Spin | 13.62 | 6.60 | 570 |
| Lost Prior Spin | 9.21 | 5.35 | 1487 |
| Overall | 10.26 | 6.09 | 2196 |

t-test, p<.0001, consistent with HH in aggregate
