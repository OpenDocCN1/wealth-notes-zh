# Rational Constrained Choice

+   - $(x_1^*, x_2^*)$ satisfies two conditions:
+   - (a) the budget is exhausted;
+     $$p_1x_1^* + p_2x_2^* = m$$
+   - (b) the slope of the budget constraint, $-p_1/p_2$, and the slope of the indifference curve containing $(x_1^*, x_2^*)$ are equal at $(x_1^*, x_2^*)$.
