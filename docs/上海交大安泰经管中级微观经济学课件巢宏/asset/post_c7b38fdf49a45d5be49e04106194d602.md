- Taking quantity demanded as given and then asking what must be price describes the inverse demand function of a commodity.

![](img/e3f3f402f74cf453caf4596d2d7d7398_265_0.png)

**A Cobb-Douglas example:**

$$
x_1^* = \frac{ay}{(a+b)p_1}
$$

is the ordinary demand function and

$$
p_1 = \frac{ay}{(a+b)x_1^*}
$$

is the inverse demand function.

**A perfect-complements example:**

$$
x_1^* = \frac{y}{p_1+p_2}
$$

is the ordinary demand function and

$$
p_1 = \frac{y}{x_1^*} - p_2
$$

is the inverse demand function.
