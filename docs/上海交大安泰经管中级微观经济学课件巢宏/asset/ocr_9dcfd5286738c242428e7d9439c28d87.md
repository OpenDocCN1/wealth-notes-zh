**Market demand**

Market quantity supplied is fixed, independent of price.  
$S(p)=c+dp$, so $d=0$ and $S(p)=c$.

$p^*=D^{-1}(q^*)$; that is,  
$p^*=(a-c)/b$.

$D^{-1}(q)=(a-q)/b$

$p^*=(a-c)/(b+d)$

$q^*=(ad+bc)/(b+d)$ with $d=0$ gives  
$p^*=(a-c)/b$

$q^*=c$

$p^*=(a-c)/b$  
$q^*=c$
