# Net Demands

+   Suppose (ω₁, ω₂) = (10, 2) and p₁=2, p₂=3. Then the constraint is p₁x₁ + p₂x₂ = p₁ω₁ + p₂ω₂ = 26.

+   If the consumer demands (x₁*, x₂*) = (7, 4), then 3 good 1 units exchange for 2 good 2 units. Net demands are x₁* - ω₁ = 7 - 10 = -3 and x₂* - ω₂ = 4 - 2 = +2.
