# Short-Run & Long-Run Total Costs

Long-run costs are:
$c(y') = w_1 x_1' + w_2 x_2'$
$c(y'') = w_1 x_1'' + w_2 x_2''$
$c(y''') = w_1 x_1''' + w_2 x_2'''$

Short-run costs are:
$c_s(y') > c(y')$
$c_s(y'') = c(y'')$
$c_s(y''') > c(y''')$
