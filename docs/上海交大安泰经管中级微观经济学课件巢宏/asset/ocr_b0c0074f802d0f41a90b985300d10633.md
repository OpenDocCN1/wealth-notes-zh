# Quantity Taxes & Market
Equilibrium

$$p_s=\frac{a-c-bt}{b+d}$$

$$q^t=\frac{ad+bc-bdt}{b+d}$$

$$p_b=\frac{a-c+dt}{b+d}$$

As $t\to0$, $p_s$ and $p_b\to\frac{a-c}{b+d}=p^*$, the equilibrium price if there is no tax ($t=0$) and $q^t\to\frac{ad+bc}{b+d}$, the quantity traded at equilibrium when there is no tax.
