## 应用：选择一种税收

- Which is better, a quantity tax or an income tax?

![](img/e3f3f402f74cf453caf4596d2d7d7398_211_0.png)

- **Quantity tax.** The original budget constraint is

$p_1x_1 + p_2x_2 = m$

If we tax the consumption of good 1 at a rate of t, it becomes

$(p_1+t)x_1 + p_2x_2 = m$

![](img/e3f3f402f74cf453caf4596d2d7d7398_212_0.png)
