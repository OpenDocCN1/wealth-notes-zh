# Misperception of Random Sequences

# Intuitive beliefs about randomness not accurate

+   - E.g. flip a (fair) coin and get 5 heads in a row
+   - tails is more likely (gambler’s fallacy)
+   - heads is more likely (reverse gambler’s fallacy, hot outcome)
+   - disposition effect in finance (Odean 1998, Shefrin and Statman 1985)

+   - E.g. betting on flips of a (fair) coin and win 5 times in a row
+   - a win is more likely (hot hand)
+   - a loss is more likely (stock of luck)
+   - choice of mutual fund managers (Sirri and Tufano 1998) (Cahart 1997)

# Two different biases

+   - Gambler’s fallacy (hot outcome)
+   - Hot hand (stock of luck)
