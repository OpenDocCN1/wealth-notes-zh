# From Individual to Market  
Demand Functions

+   ◆ **When all consumers are price-takers, the market demand function for commodity j is**

$$X_j(p_1,p_2,m^1,\ldots,m^n)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_j^{*}(p_1,p_2,m^i).$$

+   ◆ **If all consumers are identical then**

$$X_j(p_1,p_2,M)=n\times x_j^{*}(p_1,p_2,m)$$

**where** $M=nm$.
