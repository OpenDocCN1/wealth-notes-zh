"Thanks to your course," writes Robert Meecham of Toronto, "I've come from an $18-a-week job to where I'm a successful artist. I own my own house, two cars, and have money invested in two companies."

Gertrude Vander Poel had never drawn a thing until she enrolled in the school. Now a fashionable New York gallery sells her paintings.
