# PREFABRICATED HOUSING:
How Big an Industrial Giant?

By JAMES R. PRICE
President, National Homes Corporation

Let me describe a scene to you: From a charming, colorful model home, a long queue of people reaches down the street. Slowly they move through this bright, modern home—people of all ages and pocketbooks. Then many of them go to a nearby tent and sign up for a home of their own... A hundred or more houses may be sold here in a single day.

An unusual scene? No. It happens all over America, whenever a National Homes Builder-Dealer shows a model home. Families eager for better housing—anxious to get out of crowded quarters where they may be doubling up with others—throng to the opening.
