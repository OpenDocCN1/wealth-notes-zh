# ASLEEP FOR AGES—GERMS AWAKEN—FIND HAVEN IN TODAY'S BATHROOMS

By Gene Wilburn,
Medical News Writer, Houston Chronicle

While many of man's disease germs are being destroyed by the antibiotics—old germs with new destructive powers are arising, after ages of virtually peaceful existence within our organs.

Some of these organisms—Colibacillus, Proteus and Aerobacter, by name—have been helping to clear the body of waste matter in the intestines.

These "intestinal" germs have been held in check by secretions from certain disease germs like streptococcus and pneumococcus. With control of these disease germs by the antibiotics, some virulent intestinal germs are causing serious systemic infections. This adds to the problem recently created by the emergence of antibiotic resistant germs known as "staph."

All these germs can be picked up by direct contact with contaminated surfaces—for example, when deposited on bathroom floors and fixtures. Indeed, the bathroom can become a critical area for spread of disease.

That is why the bathroom cleanser should have the power to kill both "staph" and the other disease-bearing germs that may lurk in the bathroom. Published laboratory tests show that

neither soap suds nor usual pine oil solutions have the power to kill "staph"—that so-called "sanitizers" are not germicides... that is, they may not kill all the disease germs they touch.

One household product has proved to be an efficient germicide. Lysol Brand Disinfectant kills disease germs on contact—including virulent intestinal germs, "staph" and many deadly viruses. And, in laboratory tests, its germicidal action was found to last 7 full days.
