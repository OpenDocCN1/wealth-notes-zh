# 16、有些人说我们太落伍了，我们仍然认为有必要手工处理蔬菜

# Some people say
we’re old fashioned

We still think it’s necessary
to trim vegetables by hand

![](bbox=[0.17, 0.27, 0.4, 0.46])

The lady in the picture is trimming potatoes by hand.
She could just as well be working on tomatoes, carrots, turnagagas, celery, or parsnips because they all get the same treatment in the Campbell Kitchens.
Some people tell us that this seems like a terrible old-fashioned way to do things in modern kitchens like Campbell’s, and they are certainly right. The trouble is that

every vegetable is different and we have yet to find a machine that can spot and remove every last little blemish the way a keen-eyed, conscientious woman can do, with a sharp knife.
So we just go on being old fashioned. It’s the only way we can be sure that you get the goodness and nothing but the goodness, of the choice vegetables we use in Campbell’s Soups. And when you taste the soups that our skilled chefs make with these vegetables, we think you’ll agree that it wouldn’t be fair to give them anything less than the best to work with.
