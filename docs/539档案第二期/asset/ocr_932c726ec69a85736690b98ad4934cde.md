# How the switch of a horse tail helped bring eye comfort to millions

This story dates back to America’s "gay nineties" era and involves a Spokane banker, a horse and a Chicago eye specialist. In a freak accident, a spirited horse swung its tail and the coarse hairs cut the cornea of Otis F. Hall’s eye. When specialized medical care was needed, Hall went to Chicago. Accompanied by his son Frank, who was also troubled by an eye irritation, he sought out Dr. James B. McFatrich.

Within weeks their eye ailments were cured. Also Hall made an astonishing discovery. It was a special eye preparation which Dr. McFatrich compounded and regularly prescribed for his patients. Hall found it amazingly soothing and comforting for regular use after his eye ailment had been cured.

Why couldn’t it be made available to persons outside the McFatrich practice, the banker asked. The physician was reluctant, but eventually agreed. A larger number of persons could be served than by limiting the preparation only to his practice. And so the Murine Company was founded August 11, 1897, taking its

Today, Murine eyedrops are stocked in 99 percent of all drug stores throughout America and in thousands of other retail outlets, as well as in more than 60 countries. Murine is an isotonic fluid which means it blends perfectly with the natural, alkaline fluids of the eye. Thus it adds to nature’s system for cleansing the eyes by reinforcing the natural action of the tears. Murine washes away the discomforts of dust, smoke, dirt or wind. The eyes respond with a feeling of cool refreshment. By its soothing benefits, Murine relieves that "tired eye" feeling and so relaxes tension.

Today’s Murine formula, further developed, is also easier to apply. It now comes in a new leak-proof squeeze bottle as well as the familiar glass bottle with separate dropper. The eye preparation which Mr. Otis Hall found so amazingly soothing over 50 years ago, Murine, has become a household word.
