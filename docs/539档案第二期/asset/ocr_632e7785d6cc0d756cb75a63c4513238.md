# Yuma to Phoenix
204 miles ... on four
blown-out tires!

New Goodyear tires make roadside tire changes practically unnecessary after punctures or blowouts

How would you like to drive 204 miles through blast-furnace heat in a car with four blown-out tires? Doesn't sound like much fun, does it? However, a recent Goodyear test involved a car driven from Yuma to Phoenix without a moment's inconvenience even though all four tires were punctured by a cold chisel.

The trip covered 204 miles of Arizona desert where temperatures climbed higher than 120°—made roads sizzling hot.

On the lonely stretches of this road, tire failure becomes a serious hazard, but with the protection of Goodyear's built-in "spare," the test drive proceeded without difficulty.

At the end of this drive examination proved that the Captive-Air Safety tires suffered no damage to the "inner tires" and ... no damage to the outer tires other than the original chisel cuts.

The success of the Captive-Air Safety tire marks a significant advance in automotive security.
