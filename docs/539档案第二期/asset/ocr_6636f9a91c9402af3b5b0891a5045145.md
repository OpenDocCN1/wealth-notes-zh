# Rust Can Be Stopped!

When your neighbor applies a maroon red coating again over his rusty porch railing -- don't look surprised. Chances are he's stopping rust with Rust-Oleum. No ordinary coating, the basic formula of Rust-Oleum was developed nearly fifty years ago by Sea Captain Robert Fergusson, who became intrigued with the rust-stopping qualities of fish oil early in his career. Creating a special treatment for fish oil, he used the specially-processed fish oil as the vehicle in combination with other rust-inhibiting pigments. The result? A coating that stopped rust when applied over sound rusted surfaces, after scraping and wire-brushing to remove rust scale and loose rust. Possible, because the fish oil penetrated the rust to bare metal. This was the birth of Rust-Oleum's exclusive 769 Damp-Proof Red Primer.
