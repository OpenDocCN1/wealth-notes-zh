你看到过家里电线磨损严重的台灯？这可能引起严重的休克，或烧伤。确保家里所有的电线和插座都处于良好的状态。

注意到桌子上的大花瓶了吗？小孩子很容易够到……并砸伤自己。重物一定要放在桌子中间。

你家中还有哪些潜在“危险点”呢？如果你的药物放在孩子可以触摸到的地方——浴室就可能成为一个特别危险的场所。所有药品都应该保存在一个安全的地方，漂白剂，碱液，杀虫剂，清洁剂等都是如此。

## 13、为什么每个家庭都需要有家庭医生

# Why every family should have a family doctor...

![](img/8c476e4069f376783468eec04da603b3_52_0.png)

Every family should select and become acquainted with a doctor before illness strikes. Yet, many families wait until sickness or emergency requires a last-minute decision about the doctor they will call.

A family doctor, usually a general practitioner or an internist, can care for about 85 percent of your family's illnesses. And should you need specialized care, the family doctor can arrange it.

Perhaps the best way to select your doctor is through your local medical society or community hospital. They will give you the names of several physicians. You may choose one of them with assurance that you will be in good hands.

Then, call the doctor and go to see him for a friendly talk—about fees, night calls, the hospitals he is associated with, and whatever else is on your mind.

Then ask yourself: did you like him... feel at ease with him... would you trust him during those critical situations which illness often creates? These questions are important. For mutual friendship and understanding are essential to a warm doctor-patient relationship.

What are the advantages of having a regular doctor? For one thing, he will get to know you and your family intimately—your "medical history," your response to certain drugs, your abnormal blood pressure, your emotional reaction to illness, and many other facts which may be helpful when illness occurs.

He can also give you the benefits of preventive medicine. For example, if you have young children, he will want to see them at intervals to check their growth and development—and to keep their protection against communicable diseases up to date.

For others in your family, he can be a health counselor. For instance, if you're bothered at times by seemingly trivial complaints—indigestion, headache, nervousness, or fatigue—you might hesitate to go to a doctor whom you do not know. But with a family doctor, you'd feel free to talk over any condition that upsets you, now and then.

Select and become acquainted with a doctor now. Keep his address, telephone number and office hours posted in a spot known to everyone in your family. His continuing supervision can help your children grow up strong and well—and help you live a long and healthy life.

# 为什么每个家庭都需要有家庭医生...

每个家庭都应该在生病前选择一个家庭医生，并和他熟悉起来。然而，很多家庭要等到病了，或有突发状况发生，才会决定给医生打电话。

家庭医生（通常是普通科医生或内科医生），可以照顾你的家人约 85%的疾病。一旦你需要专业护理，你的家庭医生都可以提供。

选择家庭医生最好的方法是，通过当地的医疗所或社区医院。他们会给你介绍一些医生。你可以选择其中一个你可以信任的。

然后，打电话给他，约他谈谈——有关费用，夜间呼叫，和他合作的医院，等等你想了解的信息。

然后问自己：你是否喜欢他……与他相处是否感到轻松，在病情危急的情况下，你会信任他吗？这些问题都很重要。因为你们之间的友谊和理解，是建立和谐的医患关系必不可少的条件。

拥有家庭医生有哪些好处？首先，他更了解你和你的家人——了解你们的“病史”，你对某些药物的反应，你的血压状况，你对某些疾病的情绪反应，和许多其他的事实，这些信息在你们生病时，可能十分有用。

他还可以给你提供一些预防措施。例如，如果你有孩子，他会不时地检查他们的成长和发育状况，帮助他们预防传染病。

另一方面，他可以是一个健康顾问。例如，如果你偶尔有一些轻微不适——比如，消化不良、头痛、紧张，或者疲劳——你犹豫要不要去医院看医生。但是有了家庭医生，你可以随时和他咨询任何让你身体不适的问题。

现在就选择，并认识你的家庭医生吧。让你的家人都记住他的地址、电话号码和办公时间。他的持续看护，可以帮助你的孩子茁壮成长，帮助你更加健康长寿。
