# New test-tube triumph helps give your skin
THE BRIGHT LOOK OF HEALTH!

EVERYONE whose face has ever felt tight as a drumhead after a lathering with toilet soap...or itched uncomfortably after a bath...has long been a personal concern to a group of white-coated Lever Brothers researchers. But in their search for a soap that could offer healthful cleanliness with comfort, they had to re-formulate toilet soap—with hitherto unused ingredients—to create the one mild, fragrant, lathery bar that could offer this scientific skin care.

Laboratory tests, with this totally new kind of soap, proved it cleaned and cleared skin as no other could. It removed cosmetic film other soap left on skin...and left no itchy, drying soap film on skin.

In tests for mildness, no toilet soap —not even those recommended for

babies—was as mild as the new Lever Brothers triumph. This was no surprise to the scientists responsible for the new soap. It had three different reasons for mildness so outstanding that this new soap actually "agrees" with skin:
1. Its alkali balance is more closely akin to that of normal, healthy skin than any other soap. 2. Its new skin-clearing ingredient, DERMASIL, permits this unusual promise: only this new soap with DERMASIL helps clean away germs that cause blemishes and does not irritate. 3. It's richest in cold cream of any soap.

Aptly named ... this totally new kind of soap is now available for home use—to help give your skin the bright look of health! Look for it at your favorite store—the new miracle of scientific skin care, new pink PRAISE!
