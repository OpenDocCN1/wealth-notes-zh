# About Us

Powerlance was originally founded by Mehrdad Zandipour in England in 1982. Mehrdad Zandipour is a qualified engineer specializing in polymer physics. He obtained his degrees in England and ventured to Australia in 1988 where he opened his own factory inventing and creating the Powerlance product range. His goal is to manufacture the best products in the world, producing all products within Australia.
