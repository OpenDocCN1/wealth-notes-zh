上學以後，他們問我，
to wrote down what I wanted to be when I grew up,
長大後的志願夢想是什麼？
I wrote down ‘Happy.’
我寫下『快樂』兩個字。
They told me I didn’t understand the assignment,
他們說我沒搞清楚這個題目，
and I told them
我告訴他們
they didn’t understand LIFE.
是他們沒搞清楚人生。

------ John Lennon, (1940~1980) Musician
