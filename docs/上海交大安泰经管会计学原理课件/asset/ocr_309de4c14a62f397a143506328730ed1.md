# Dividend Yield

Dividend
Yield Ratio = Dividends Per Share
Market Price Per Share

Babson Builders pays an annual dividend of $1.50 per share of capital stock. The market price of the company’s capital stock was $15.25 at the end of 2009.

This ratio identifies the return, in terms of cash dividends, on the current market price of the stock.
