# Ford 2011 10-k:
Discontinued Operations

+   On August 2, 2010, we completed the sale of Volvo and related assets. As agreed, Volvo will retain or acquire certain assets presently used by Volvo,

+   The total purchase price for Volvo and the Related Assets set forth in the agreement was $1.8 billion, of which $200 million was to be paid in the form of a note and the balance to be paid in cash, with the cash portion subject to customary purchase price adjustments.

+   recognized a pre-tax loss of $23 million reported in Automotive interest income and other non-operating income/(expense), net.

+   This loss includes the recognition of $1.5 billion of accumulated other comprehensive income and $38 million of related separation costs. We expect to finalize and settle the final true-up of the purchase price adjustments in the first quarter of 2011.
