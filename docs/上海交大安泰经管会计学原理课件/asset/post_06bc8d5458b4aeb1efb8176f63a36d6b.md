## 调账

当收入和费用的影响
超过一个会计期间，
就需要编制调整分录

每个调整分录会涉及到
revenue or expense
and an asset or
liability的变化

## 调账

调整跨期交易与事项

### 调账的框架

调整

Paid (or received) cash *before* expense (or revenue) recognized

Paid (or received) cash *after* expense (or revenue) recognized

Prepaid (Deferred) expenses*

Unearned (Deferred) revenues

Accrued expense

Accrued revenues

*including depreciation

## 调账前后分录

| | 支出现金（收到现金） | 确认费用（确认收入） |
|---|---|---|
| 递延（Deferred） | 借：待摊费用（递延费用）<br>贷：现金 | 借：费用<br>贷：待摊费用（递延费用） |
|  | 借：现金<br>贷：预收账款（递延收入） | 借：预收账款（递延收入）<br>贷：收入 |

| | 确认费用（确认收入） | 支出现金（收到现金） |
|---|---|---|
| 应计（Accrued） | 借：费用<br>贷：预提费用（应计费用） | 借：预提费用（应计费用）<br>贷：现金 |
|  | 借：资产（应收账款）<br>贷：收入（应计收入） | 借：现金<br>贷：资产（应收账款） |

## 调整的类型

- ★ Converting assets to expenses
- ★ 资产转为费用

- ⏱ Converting liabilities to revenue
- ⏱ 负债转为收入

- ⏱ Accruing unpaid expenses
- ⏱ 未支付的费用

- ⏱ Accruing uncollected revenues
- ⏱ 未收讫的收入

## 如何调整

| Type | Balance Sheet Account | Income Statement Account | Adjusting Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prepaid Expenses** | Asset Overstated<br>Equity Overstated | Expense Understated | Dr. Expense<br>Cr. Asset |
| **Unearned Revenues** | Liability Overstated<br>Equity Understated | Revenue Understated | Dr. Liability<br>Cr. Revenue |
| **Accrued Expenses** | Liability Understated<br>Equity Overstated | Expense Understated | Dr. Expense<br>Cr. Liability |
| **Accrued Revenues** | Asset Understated<br>Equity Understated | Revenue Understated | Dr. Asset<br>Cr. Revenue |

## 结账

- Resets revenue, expense and withdrawal account balances to **zero** at the end of the period.
- Helps summarize a period’s revenues and expenses in the **Income Summary** account.
