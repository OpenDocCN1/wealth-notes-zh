# 编制现金流量表

+   Preparing a statement of cash flows involves five steps:
+   1. Compute the net increase or decrease in cash;
+   2. Compute and report net cash provided or used by operating activities;
+   3. Compute and report net cash provided or used by investing activities;
+   4. Compute and report net cash provided or used by financing activities;
+   5. Compute the net cash flow by combining net cash provided or used by operating, investing, and financing activities and then prove it by adding it to the beginning cash balance to show that it equals the ending cash balance.
