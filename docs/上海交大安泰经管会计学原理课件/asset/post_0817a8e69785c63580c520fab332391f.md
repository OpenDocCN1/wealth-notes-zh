## Profit Margin

\[
\text{Profit margin} = \frac{\text{Net income}}{\text{Net sales}}
\]

This ratio describes a company's ability to earn profit from each sales dollar.

## Return on Assets (ROA)

This ratio is generally considered the best overall measure of a company's profitability.

![](img/a2796f05ebe2fd31075702d18360d28c_187_0.png)

```
ROA = Net income ÷ Average total assets
    = $53,690 ÷ [($300,000 + $346,390) ÷ 2]
    = 16.61%
```

## Return on Equity (ROE)

![](img/a2796f05ebe2fd31075702d18360d28c_188_0.png)

This measure indicates how well the company employed the owners' investments to earn income.

```
ROE = Net income ÷ Average total equity
    = $53,690 ÷ [($180,000 + $234,390) ÷ 2]
    = 25.91%
```

## Earnings Per Share

\[
\text{EPS} = \frac{\text{Net income}}{\text{Average shares of capital stock outstanding}}
\]

Look back at the information from Babson and get the values we need to calculate earnings per share.

\[
\frac{\$53,690}{27,400} = \$1.96
\]

![](img/a2796f05ebe2fd31075702d18360d28c_190_0.png)

## Market-Based Measures

### Price-Earnings Ratio

![](img/a2796f05ebe2fd31075702d18360d28c_191_0.png)

\[
\frac{\text{Current market price of one share of stock}}{\text{Earnings per share}} = \text{P/E}
\]

\[
\frac{\$15.25}{\$1.96} = 7.78
\]

The measure shows us the relationship between earnings of the company and the market price of its stock.

![](img/a2796f05ebe2fd31075702d18360d28c_192_0.png)

### Dividend Yield

\[
\text{Dividend Yield Ratio} = \frac{\text{Dividends per share}}{\text{Market price per share}}
\]

Babson Builders pays an annual dividend of $1.50 per share of capital stock. The market price of the company's capital stock was $15.25 at the end of 2009.

This ratio identifies the return, in terms of cash dividends, on the current market price of the stock.

## Economic Value Added

**What is it?**

**Why is it important?**

**Why is it called *residual income*?**

**Defined:**

```
Economic Value Added = Operating Income* - [Cost of Capital × Total Capitalization]
```
