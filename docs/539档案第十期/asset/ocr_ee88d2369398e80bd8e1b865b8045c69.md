# 5、史无前例，超低价特别报告

# Financial Intelligence Reports

210 Avenue 1, Suite C
Redondo Beach, CA 9027

October 24, 1984

I have a tax problem and I want you to be the beneficiary instead of the IRS...

Dear Valued Friend:

My business has had a great year so far in 1984. In fact, we've done so well I'm probably going to pay 2½ times more corporate taxes than I did last year. And if we make any more money this year the tax problem will only be worse — and I'll have to pay even more money to the IRS.

# herein lies part of my problem.

You see I have employees who I don't want to lay-off. I also have a lot of newsletters I committed to sell before the year is over — as well as 5,000 various special situation reports I have committed to market.

# What this all boils down to is simple:

I've got to generate a lot more business -- both to keep my employees on the payroll and meet my commitments to the publisher, but if I sell it at a profit, my tax problem becomes even worse. So I've decided on a strategy that's so bold — so daring — that I doubt anyone else would dare try it.

I have decided to offer you newsletters and special reports and only charge you the basic cost to us. Just enough to pay the publisher and the associated cost necessary to process your order — and not a dime more.

(This comes to just $13-25 on the newsletters — they normally cost $77-250 — and $10 or less on the reports. About 85-90% off normal prices.)

In other words I'm going to offer you newsletters and reports for no personal profit whatsoever. All I ask is that you cover the real cost I have in getting them into your hands.
