I've been able to produce similar increases for hundreds of other retailers with stores like yours. Many of these people would be glad to share their experience with you.

+   1. Have Christmas shoppers lined up at your door waiting for your opening.

4. Generate heavy cash flow to cover those invoices for fall and winter merchandise that are normally due on the 10th of December.

6. Gain the advantage in your market. Get customers coming in, buying, and coming back before you become lost in the excessive holiday advertising of the other merchants in your area.
7. Create excitement among your customers and your employees which will make their holiday season more fun—and yours more profitable.
