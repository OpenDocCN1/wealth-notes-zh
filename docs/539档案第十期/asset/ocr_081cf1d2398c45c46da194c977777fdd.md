# How it Feels to Earn $1000 a Week

By a Young Man Who Four Years Ago Drew a $25 a Week Salary. Tells How He Accomplished It.

How does it feel to earn $1000 a week? How does it feel to have earned $300,000 in four years? How does it feel to be free from money worries? How does it feel to have everything one can want? These are questions I shall answer for the benefit of my reader out of my own personal experience. And I shall try to explain, simply and clearly the secret of what my friends call my phenomenal success.

Let me begin four years ago. At that time my wife and I and our two babies were living on my earnings of twenty-five dollars a week. We occupied a tiny flat, wore the plainest clothes, had the cheapest food and the cheapest entertainment—and dreamed of the day when I might be earning fifty dollars a week. That was the limit of my ambition. Indeed, it seemed to be the limit of my possibilities. For I was but an average man, without influential friends, without a liberal education, without a dominating personality, and without money.

With nothing to begin with, I have become the sole owner of a business which has paid me over $300,000 in four years during the past four years and which now pays me more than a thousand dollars a week. I did not gamble. I did not make my money in Wall Street. My business is not a "get rich quick" scheme. On the contrary, many others in my line have failed since the war began.

In four years the entire scheme of my life has changed. Instead of living in a two by four flat, we occupy our own home, built for it at a cost of over $30,000. We have three automobiles. Our children go to private schools. We have everything things we want, and we want the best of everything. I estimate that my income for the past year was $100,000, or in terms of a million dollars—with greater possibilities of my dream coming true than my former dream of earning fifty dollars a week.

What brought about this remarkable change? What transformed me, almost overnight, from a slow-going, easily-sheepish, average man—into a positive, quick-acting, determined individual who attacks no defeat, who overcomes every obstacle, and who completely dominates every situation? It all began with a question my wife asked me one evening after reading an article in a magazine about a great engineer who was said to earn a $10,000 salary.

"How do you suppose it feels to earn $1000 a week?" she asked. And without thinking, I replied "I haven't the slightest idea, my dear, so the only way to find out is to earn it." We both laughed, and soon the question was apparently forgotten.

But that night, and for weeks afterward, the same question and my wife's keen rejoinder ate my brain. I commenced to examine the men of enterprise in our town. What is it that enables them to get everything they want? They are not better educated than I—at least, some are less intelligent. But the fact remains that they are men of success. I lacked the one thing that was their real asset. I perceived that they looked at things from an entirely different angle than I. Whatever it was, that "something" was the secret of their success. I was the only thing that prevented me from achieving success, a lack of money-making ability. In all other ways we were the same.

Determined to find out what that vital spark of success was, I began to search every subject that pertained to the matter. I followed one idea after another. But I, I found nothing. I almost despaired. I came across a copy of "Power of Will." Like a bolt from a clear sky there flashed in my brain the secret I had been seeking. That was the fundamental principle of all success—Power of Will. There was the power for which I lacked, and which every successful man possesses.

"Power of Will" was written by Prof. Frank Channing Haddock, a scientist, whose name ranks with such leaders of thought as James, Bergson and Kipling. After twenty years of research and study, he had completed the most thorough and systematic study of will-power ever made. I was astonished to read his statement that, "The will is just as amenable of development as the muscles of the body." And Dr. Haddock had actually set down the very rules, lessons and exercises by which anyone could develop the will, making it a bigger, stronger force each day, simply through an easy, progressive course of training.

It is almost needless to say that I at once began to familiarize myself with the book. I studied it carefully. And I need not mention the extraordinary results that I obtained almost from the first day. Shortly after that, I took hold of a business that for twelve years had been losing money. I started with $300 of borrowed capital. During my first year I made $30,000. My second year paid me $80,000. My third year netted me $100,000. Last year, due to increased costs of materials, my profits were $100,000, though my volume of business increased 25%. And the year I am facing through will bring my profits for the present fiscal year up to $125,000.

Earning a thousand dollars a week makes me feel secure against want. It gives me the money with which to buy anything I want. It will make my family happy. It has made me a co-operation of an investment that looks good, without worrying about losing the interest. It has given me mind of financial worries. It has made me healthier, more contented and keener minded. It is the grandest recipe I know for happiness.
