Why we have been willing to lose money on this introductory offer—and why it must soon be withdrawn

Yet they are given free—in order to introduce the NEW set of thirty Little Leather Library world's masterpieces. We know what has happened in the past on our previous sets after they were introduced—orders poured in by the thousands. The wisest thing we could do, therefore, was to introduce this NEW set QUICKLY into representative homes. We know what will happen after this is done. Every set will become a 'silent salesman,' more powerful than any other form of advertising we could do.

That is the reason—the only reason—we have been willing to lose money on this offer.
