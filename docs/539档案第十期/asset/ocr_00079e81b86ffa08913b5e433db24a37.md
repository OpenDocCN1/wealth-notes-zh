# My Boss Would Kill Me If He Found Out I Was Writing You This Letter...
But I Want To Prove A Point To Him!

Dear Mr. Mundale:

I work for a man many people believe is a marketing genius. I can't say for sure - but I do know that his marketing accomplishments are usually amazing. I've worked for him a short 15 months - yet in that time I've actually witnessed him save ten struggling companies from near certain failure - with simple little tricks that were so logical I hit myself for not thinking of them.

But you know Mr. Mundale, that's his greatest talent. The man's so extraordinarily logical. And frankly, few entrepreneurs approach their marketing problems in a logical, common-sense, pragmatic manner.

But my boss Jay Abraham does. And it produces miraculous results from companies that never believed they had so much untapped profit potential in them.

In 1981, Jay Abraham was personally responsible for just under one billion dollars in sales volume that resulted directly from breakthrough marketing techniques he produced.

Jay has a very prosperous practice. Four clients earn him well over a quarter million dollars a year. And he sits in his office right outside his home in the seaside city of Palos Verdes, California, where he never wears anything but tee shirts and shorts. And never ever leaves home! Can you believe this, all four of Jay's clients come to him. From New York, Minnesota, Utah and San Francisco. Jay communicates with them all via telecopier and Watts line. He hasn't even visited two of his four clients' businesses.
