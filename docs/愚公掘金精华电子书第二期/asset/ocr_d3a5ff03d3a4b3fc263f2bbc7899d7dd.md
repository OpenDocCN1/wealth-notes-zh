 Although we have a bail trial system, but in specific execution, there are no very detailed regulations.

Whether or not to grant bail, the power of decision is still in the hands of the courts and public security bureaus.

Imagine, you are the police, now the largest local tax-paying enterprise has sued an employee, although after investigation, no evidence was found, but what if this person is indeed guilty?

After all, detaining him all along does not violate any regulations, why take the risk of releasing him? Better to keep him within the scope of control and investigate slowly...

This is the core issue in the Huawei case, which has been overlooked by many people.
