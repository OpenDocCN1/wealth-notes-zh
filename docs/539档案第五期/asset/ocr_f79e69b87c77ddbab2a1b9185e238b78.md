For over 30 years the formula was tested and improved into a plant food that nutritionally was great for grass and other plants. But the mechanics of feeding grass has always been a problem. You see, grass is fed by having the entire meal dumped on top of its head. And unless the plant foods were watered in, they would burn.

The reason for the burn is part of a phenomenon called osmosis. In plain language it works like this: A plant food that dissolves in water does so because it’s "thirsty."

When dropped on leaves, the plant foods soak up every drop of water they can get. But there’s never enough to go around. So they pull water right out of the leaves. And that’s the beginning of burn.

In the past, the only ways to hold down burn were to give the grass a bath or raking after feeding... or feed the grass in "watered-down" nibbles every month or so.

But Vigoro scientists weren’t satisfied. They wanted a formula so burn-free it could be fed in amounts large enough to last a full season—with no watering in required.

Why? Because every burn—even a slight scorch that goes unnoticed—is a setback. And setbacks lay the grass wide open to attack from weeds, sun, insects, disease.

With the help of an instrument called an isoteniscope, the scientists measured the thirst of every plant food ingredient and experimented with new compounds that could replace the thirsty ones.

These tests finally paid off in a brand-new formula with every ingredient in a nonburning form. And on March 18, 1958, U.S. Patent Number 2,827,358 was awarded to this amazing product called New Golden Vigoro—the first, complete, non-burning plant food ever made.
