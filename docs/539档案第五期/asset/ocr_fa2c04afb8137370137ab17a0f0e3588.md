# Fluorides for Healthy Teeth

NEXT TO THE COMMON COLD, dental decay is man's most widespread chronic disease. But now Science has accomplished a major breakthrough against dental decay by using fluoride these three ways: 1. fluoridation of drinking water; 2. direct topical application, by dentists, of a fluoride solution to the teeth; 3. stannous fluoride in tooth paste.

You are the one who chooses the fluoride tooth paste you will use. And here are some surprising facts which can guide you: (1) Not all fluoride tooth pastes contain stannous fluoride, the recognized decay-fighting ingredient. (2) Some fluoride tooth pastes can lose as much as 80% of their active fluoride right in the tube. (3) Only one tooth paste on the market today provides the most favorable "climate" in your mouth for the stannous fluoride to work best.
