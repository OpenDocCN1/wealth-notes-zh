# Why Sears signed Ted Williams
-as a playing manager

As a Sears, Roebuck and Co. consultant, Ted Williams brings to his new job the same keen eye and straight talk that set him apart as a ballplayer. Read how he helps Sears field-test and improve its sports equipment—in order to give you more for your money.

Why did Sears hire Ted Williams?
For one simple reason. To add a cold, professional viewpoint on the quality of every piece of Sears sports equipment before it gets into the Sears catalog or any one of the 740 Sears department stores.
Williams is a working consultant with a very important job. He helps select the sports equipment Sears sells. He personally field-tests it. And he suggests improvements in order to keep Sears a stride ahead in the sporting goods field.
For instance, he may recommend putting bigger, stronger wheels on a camp trailer, lighter soles on a pair of hunting boots. Softer and thinner leather inside the liner of a fielder’s glove—to create the proper “feel.”

Making sure its products really work has always been a main concern of Sears. That’s the reason behind the $50,000,000 Sears laboratory for testing all kinds of merchandise.
And that’s the reason for hiring Ted Williams. If the legendary Williams’ batting eye can’t detect a flaw, Sears knows the item is absolutely first-rate.
Ted Williams’ name on any sports equipment you buy at Sears is not just an empty endorsement.
It means that your judgment in choosing this product is backed by a man who would never lend his name to anything he wouldn’t use himself.

Ted Williams’ name is no longer in the box scores. But you’ll find it on a wide range of sports items that are available only at Sears.
