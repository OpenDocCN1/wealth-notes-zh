# The story
of the first
FOOLPROOF
LAWN FOOD

Almost from the first day the Pilgrims landed, Americans have tried to make two blades of grass grow where one grew before.
It all started when Squanto, a friendly Wampanoag Indian, showed the early settlers how to plant a fish in every hill of corn.
Since this crude beginning, thousands of scientists have labored to produce plant foods that are more productive and easier to use.
Vigoro plant nutritionists, for example, have devoted 35 years to the feeding of lawns and gardens.
Recently, they uncovered amazing secrets by "talking" to grass.
This resulted in what plant sci-

entists call the first "isotonic" plant food. For unscientific homeowners this adds up to the first foolproof lawn food.
The story really began when the Vigoro scientists first learned to "listen" to plants. They grew seedlings in pots of sterile Sahara Desert sand enriched with different combinations of plant foods. The plants talked back in terms of growth and the scientists learned exactly what diet was needed.
