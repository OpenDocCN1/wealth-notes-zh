# The Ice Follies' most punished performer

![](bbox=[0.15, 0.31, 0.32, 0.5])

Words of wisdom for the sports enthusiast from Eddie Shipstad of Shipstads & Johnson, creators of the world's first fabulous ice extravaganza.

"Behind every breath-catching spin, every precision waltz, every split second of gymnastic slapstick you see, there are grueling long hours of practice, and countless spills on wet, rough, ammoniated ice. Offhand, I can't imagine any other instance where clothes are subjected to such constant punishment and still have to look perfect. For instance, one of our skaters wore the same formal dress suit more than 800 times in two years! Not many suits could take this. But we've learned that a fine wool suit can stand 800 to 1,000 wearings and still look as though it had arrived from the tailors that very day. "One reason for this, our famous designer Ronnie tells me, is that the wool fiber is uniquely resilient. A skater can bend, stretch, twist, turn, fall, and slide on the ice—and his wool suit won't lose its shape, that or wear thin. This remarkable elasticity of wool not only gives the performer more freedom for action and trimmer appearance, it saves us money. With wool, care and replacement costs are at a minimum. "Experience has shown us, we can count on wool for showmanship. Pure wool clothes can take month after month of tough wear and still show up for every performance in the pink of condition. And they keep their fresh, bright colors for years!" Well, it goes without saying that, if wool can take this kind of treatment from professional ice skaters, it will do even more for you. Pants, jackets, sweaters for skaters and skiers... rugged hunting clothes... winter suits and coats of pure American-made woollens and worsteds will all give you unequalled performance. And because the crimpy wool fiber makes these wools self-insulating, you get never-failing warmth, too... without extra bulk to weigh you down. Whether you're the occasional weekender or dedicated sportsman, you'll find that for comfort, long wear, and impressive appearance, there's no doubt about it, nothing measures up to wool.
