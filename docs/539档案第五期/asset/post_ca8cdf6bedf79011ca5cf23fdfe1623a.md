
## 关于心脏病的一些事实和谬论

心脏病的康复几率正在稳步增加。

正确——不久之前，大多数人都觉得心脏病或“冠心病”通常都意味着突然早逝——或过着病弱的生活。

现在我们知道，大多数心脏病人——约 80%——首次心脏病发作都康复了，很多都康复的非常好，又幸福地生活了好多年。

康复后，大多数冠状动脉患者都回去工作了。

正确——康复得好的病人，在合适的条件下工作，都不会出现行为不便的情况。给病人安排一些对心脏负荷不是很大的工作，并给予适当的医疗监督，他就可以不受伤害地正常工作。事实上，大多数医生都鼓励患者重返工作岗位。

心脏病发作，是剧烈运动引起的。

错误——很多心脏病专家现在都认为，体力消耗与心脏病发作的关系很小。事实上，心脏病发作，通常发生在睡眠或休息的时候。

很多因素——包括年龄、性别、职业和饮食——对冠心病都有影响。

康复后的冠心病患者，应该减肥，控制自己的体重。

正确——继良好的医疗护理之后，对冠心病患者来说，没有什么比把体重保持在一个理想的水平，更重要了。

每增加 10 磅的体重，都会给你的心脏带来 10 磅的负担。

有过一次心脏病的人，肯定还会有下一次。

错误——康复后的病人，不应该活在一直担心他还会犯心脏病的恐惧中。他可能会犯也可能不会再犯心脏病。

坚持按照医生建议的饮食生活，控制体重，正常作息，很多康复后的心脏病人，都没有再犯过病。

## "Mother and baby doing fine"

Wonderful progress has been made in protecting the lives of mothers and babies—thanks to excellent prenatal care and improved obstetrical techniques.

Equally important, some 95 percent of today's babies are born in our hospitals—where most birth emergencies can be handled promptly and effectively.

Information for expectant mothers: If your general health is good—if you have no family history of diabetes, kidney, heart or other serious diseases—the chances are excellent that no major complications will occur before your baby's arrival.

For your own and your baby's welfare, however, your physician may suggest these safeguards:

- 1. Eat a variety of foods. Your baby will be no stronger or sturdier than the building materials your diet provides. For you and your baby, a varied diet is best.
- 2. Watch your weight. Too much weight strains the heart and other organs. Normal increase is around 16 to 20 pounds. Your doctor will decide how much you should gain.
- 3. Get needed rest and exercise. Walking is one of the best exercises and it's usually all right to play golf and dance in the middle months of pregnancy.
- Get plenty of sleep and at least an hour of relaxation every afternoon.
- 4. Keep in touch with your doctor. No serious problem develops during pregnancy without a warning signal. Your doctor can foresee and act to avoid difficulties—if he's consulted early and as often as necessary.
- 5. Make your hospital reservation early. The maternity wards of most hospitals are crowded nowadays. The sooner you make your reservation, the better.

## "母子平安"

现在高超的产前保健和助产技术，使孕妇和孩子获得了更好的保护。

给孕妇的一些信息：如果你的整体健康状况比较好——没有糖尿病家族史、肾脏，心脏或其他严重疾病——那么在宝宝出生之前，就不会出现重大并发症。

然而，为了你和孩子的健康，医生还是会给你一些保护性建议：

- 1. 饮食多样化。没有什么比你在饮食中提供的多样化营养食物，能让宝宝更健康，更茁壮成长了。为了你和你的宝贝，多样化的饮食是最好的选择。
- 2. 控制体重。体重过重，会给心脏和其他器官带来压力。正常的体重增长是 16—20 磅。医生会告诉你，你应该增长多少体重。

- 4. 与医生保持联系。怀孕期间，在严重的问题发生前，都会有让人警觉的信号。如果提前经常咨询医生，他就可以预见并采取行动，来避免一些问题。
- 5. 提前预定医院。现在大部分的医院产科病房都非常拥挤。所以，越早预定越好。

## How to “chill-proof”
your children

Some helpful advice about clothes from Beulah France, R.N.: child care specialist and author of “How to Have a Happy Child”

A young neighbor of mine is having a miserable childhood, just because his mother doesn’t know how to dress little boys for snow! All winter long I hear her calling: “Be careful, Peter. Don’t get wet, Peter. Don’t stay out too long, Peter. Don’t … don’t … don’t, Peter. You’ll catch cold.”

Unfortunately, she may be right. Too often winter fun does end in a siege in bed, because many times a child who looks properly dressed for the cold is not. Healthy dressing is not so much a matter of how many clothes you put on a youngster, but what clothes you select.

My years devoted to child care have taught me that wool clothing lets children have the carefree times they deserve, while giving them the best possible health protection. And there are good reasons why this is so, as I learned when I studied textiles in college.

To begin with, wool traps and holds insulating air that shuts out cold, locks in warmth—gives continuous comfort and protection, regardless of weather. Pure wool can “take” even the wetness of a snowdrift—for each wool fiber has its own “raincoat” that makes the fabric naturally water-repellent.

At the same time, wool prevents perspiration (often caused by lively play) from collecting next to the skin, because there are tiny pores in each wool fiber that can absorb moisture vapor. That’s why, with clothing of wool, your child is protected from the clammy dampness and the shock of rapid cooling that can so often lead to “catching cold.”

Besides safeguarding health, today’s American-made woolens and worsteds give remarkable comfort and durability. Thanks to the natural elasticity of the wool fiber, pure wool clothes give a child unhampered freedom for play. And coats, suits, slacks and sweaters of wool pass all the endurance tests an active child can give them, year after year. When it comes to dressing your youngsters wisely, nothing measures up to wool.
