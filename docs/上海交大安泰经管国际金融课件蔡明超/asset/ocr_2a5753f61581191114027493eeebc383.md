# Libor

Bookmark this page | Email this page

The BBA LIBOR is the most widely used benchmark or reference rate for short term interest rates. It is compiled by the BBA and released to the market at about 11.00 each day. LIBOR stands for the London Interbank Offered Rate and is the rate of interest at which banks borrow funds from other banks, in marketable size, in the London interbank market.
