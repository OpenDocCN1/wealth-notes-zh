# 百度现金流简化分析

FCFF=cash flow from operations less capital expenditures
Firms that generate a free cash flow margin (free cash flow divided by total revenue) above 5% are usually considered cash cows.
Baidu's free cash flow margin has averaged about 43.2% during the past 3 years.
