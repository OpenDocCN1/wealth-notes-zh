# European&American Currency Options
欧式与美式

+   - European-style currency options are similar to American-style options except that they can only be exercised on the expiration date.
+   - For firms that purchase options to hedge future cash flows, this loss in terms of flexibility is probably not an issue (如果公司未来某天收到一笔外汇，则欧式与美式期权下的套保并无差异). Hence, if their premiums are lower, European-style currency options may be preferred. (如果欧式比美式便宜，公司更愿选择欧式
