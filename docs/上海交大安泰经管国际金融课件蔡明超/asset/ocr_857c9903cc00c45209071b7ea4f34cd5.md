# Factors Affecting FDI
资本与金融账户下影响FDI的因素

+   - Changes in Restrictions（管制放松）
+     - New opportunities may arise from the removal of government barriers.
+   - Privatization私有化
+     - FDI has also been stimulated by the selling of government operations.
+   - Potential Economic Growth经济增长
+     - Countries with higher potential economic growth are more likely to attract FDI.
