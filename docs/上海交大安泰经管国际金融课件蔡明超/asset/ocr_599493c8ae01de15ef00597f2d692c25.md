# Factors Affecting FDI

+   - Tax Rates税率
+     - Countries that impose relatively low tax rates on corporate earnings are more likely to attract FDI.

+   - Exchange Rates汇率预期升值
+     - Firms will typically prefer to invest their funds in a country when that country’s currency is expected to strengthen.
