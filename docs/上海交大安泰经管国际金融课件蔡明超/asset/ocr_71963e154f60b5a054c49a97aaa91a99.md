# Eurocredit Market
什么是欧洲信贷市场

+   - Loans of one year or longer are extended by Eurobanks to MNCs or government agencies in *Eurocredit market*. The loans are known as *Eurocredit loans*. 一年以上的欧洲美元贷款，贷款利率基准LIBOR+信用
+   - Floating rates are commonly used, since the banks’ asset and liability maturities may not match - Eurobanks accept short-term deposits but sometimes provide longer term loans. （贷款以浮动利率LIBOR+信用溢价为主，浮动原因：解决存贷期限不匹配。）
