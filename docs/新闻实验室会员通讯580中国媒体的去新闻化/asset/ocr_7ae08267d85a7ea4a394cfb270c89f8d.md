# Notes

+   1. Discursive autonomy for Chinese journalists, however limited, is a function of negotiations between central and local governments (see: Tong, 2010; Stockmann, 2013). Therefore, in-  creased reliance on state media indicates the re-centralization of control.  
    2. “Edge-balling” is a tactical move commonly practiced by journalists to sidestep censorship through coating politically sensitive or hostile materials with legitimate rhetoric expressions.
