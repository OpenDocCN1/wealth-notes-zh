Reuters (London) - Lagarde, the head of the European Central Bank, said on Thursday the need to strengthen the euro as the currency continues to fall against the US dollar.

The euro hit its lowest level against the dollar in six months as investors sought safe-haven assets amid global economic uncertainty.

Lagarde said that the euro's value is currently defined by investors selling dollar assets and buying European bonds and other safe assets, which has exposed the European market to volatility risks in capital flows.

She added that the current situation is not sustainable and that the euro should not be forced to be a safe haven.

The euro fell to 1.0533 dollars per dollar on Thursday, its lowest level since April 2023, as markets weighed the prospects of interest rate cuts by central banks.
