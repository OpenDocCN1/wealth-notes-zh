## What is Sampling?
![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_58_0.png)

- A population (总体) is the collection of all the elements of interest.
- A sample (样本) is a subset of the population.

## Why Sampling?
- In practice, the complete data of population is **hard to acquire** due to undesirable cost of data collecting.
- **Sampling (抽样)** aims at collecting partial data of population to make an **inference** about population characteristics.

![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_59_0.png)

## Sampling Error
- With **proper sampling** methods, the **sample results** will provide “good” estimates of the population parameters.

![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_61_0.png)

- In other words, we want to minimize the sampling error.

## Sampling Category
Probability sampling: The element in population has an equal chance of getting sampled.

- Simple random sampling (简单随机抽样)
- Stratified random sampling (分层随机抽样)
- Cluster sampling (整支抽样化聚簇抽样)
- Systematic sampling (系统)

Non-probability sampling (非概率): The element in population is based on the subjective judgment or convenience of the researcher rather than random selection.

- Convenience Sampling: by convenience
- Judgment Sampling: by expertise

## Simple Random Sampling
Simple Random Sampling: Given sample size n and population size N, each possible sample of size n has the same probability of being selected.

Example (美纸纸纸): 假设该行校为 乱三9000复学生，我们想从中抽取1200复学生，若概何在简单纯为抽样？

1. Assign the students the numbers 1 to 9000.
2. Generate a table of random numbers.
3. Select random numbers from the table in groups of four digits.
4. Discard the number if it is larger than 9000.

See the following table for random numbers.

## Simple Random Sampling
- We may start the selection of random numbers anywhere in the table and move systematically in a direction of our choice:
  - For example, we start from 9986 (i.e., the last four digits of the second number). It is greater than 9000 and is discarded.
- Then, the following four digits (i.e., 7174) are smaller than 9000 and thus are selected.

![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_65_0.png)

- Note: When we refer to simple random sampling, we will assume the sampling is without replacement.

## Simple Random Sampling
- How about if the population is infinitely large?

- 释义：概率我们在度打劳对百客四六行简单随机为抽样，百客的数量发格丸呢，那么我们可以随为抽取1200复百客?

1. Each element must be selected comes from the same population
   - e.g., sampling from people who make a purchase as some may just come to use the restroom.

2. Each element is selected independently
   - e.g., whenever a customer presented a discount coupon (randomly and independently of other customers), the next customer served was selected.

## Stratified Random Sampling (分层随机抽样)
1. The elements in the population are first divided into groups called strata according to some features.
2. After the strata are formed, a simple random sample is taken from each stratum.
