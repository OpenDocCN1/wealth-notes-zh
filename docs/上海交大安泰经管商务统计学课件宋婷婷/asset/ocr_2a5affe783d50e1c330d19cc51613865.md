# Tabular Displays

Categorical variable

+   - Categorical data: Data that can be grouped by specific categories
+   - Tabular display methods:
+     - Frequency Distribution (频数表)
+     - Relative Frequency Distribution (相对频数分布)
+     - Percent Frequency Distribution (百分数频数表)
+   - Example: Guests staying at Yaduo Resort were asked to rate the quality of their accommodations as being excellent, above average, average, below average, or poor.
+   - A sample of 20 guests' rating results

| Below Average | 5 | 0.25 | 25 |
| Average | 6 | 0.30 | 30 |
| Above Average | 6 | 0.30 | 30 |
