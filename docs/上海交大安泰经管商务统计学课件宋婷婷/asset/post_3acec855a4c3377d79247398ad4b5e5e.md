## The History of Statistics

Biometrika: Galton and Pearson founded Biometrika as the first journal of statistics in 1901, and the latter founded the world's first statistics department at UCL.

The Beginning of Ancient Statistics (B.C. 450): "Three Counting Problems"

The "Probability" Foundations for Statistics in the 16th-17th Century: The probability concept and theory from gambling

"Paradigm Reform and Innovation" in Modern Statistics (1900-2000): The Fourth Paradigm: data-intensive scientific discovery

The Growth of Ancient Statistics (A.D. 2): The Han Dynasty of China conducted the first accurate census in human history

"Four Great Discoveries" of Statistical Science in the 18th-19th Century: Law of Large Numbers, Bayes' theorem, analysis of errors, regression and correlation

Source: 郁东, 刘东升, 刘爱霞, 统计的历史与统计学发展. 2017.

## Summary: Statistics (统计学)

### 1 Descriptive Statistics (描述统计学)

- Statistics is used to summarize and to present the data.
- Limitation: Only concerned with properties of the observed data, say nothing about the population.

### 2 Statistical Inference (统计推断)

- Require information about population, while data can only be collected from a sample.
  - Population: the set of all elements of interest in a particular study
  - Sample: the subset of the population
- Use data from a sample ⇒ infer properties of a population
  - Derive estimates: mean, variance, confidence intervals
  - Conducting hypotheses tests: parametric (nonparametric) testing, ANOVA, regression, Experiment design
