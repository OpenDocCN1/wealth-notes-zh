# Example

+   - A company's personnel director wants to determine if there are differences in the performance ratings among the managers who graduated from three colleges
+   - The hypotheses are
+     * H₀ : All populations are identical in terms of performance
+     * Hₐ : Not all populations are identical in terms of performance
+   - We will use a 0.05 level of significance for the test.
+   - Independent samples of performance rating data are available
