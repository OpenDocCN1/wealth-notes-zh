# Sampling Distribution of \bar{x}

+   - **Form of the Sampling Distribution of \bar{x}**
+     1. The population has a normal distribution
+        - The sampling distribution of \bar{x} is normally distributed for any sample size.
+     2. The population does not have a normal distribution
+        - We need the sample size to be large, then the sampling distribution of \bar{x} is approximately normal (Central Limit Theorem).
