# Terminology of A/B tests

+   - Terminology of A/B tests:
+     - **Overall Evaluation Criterion (OEC) (综合评估指标)**: A quantitative measure of the experiment's objective, also called the *response or dependent variable (采测变量)*
+     - **Parameter (参数)**: A controllable experimental variable that is thought to influence the OEC or other metrics of interest, sometimes also called *factors or variables (因子)*
+     - **Variant (变体)**: A user experience being tested, typically by assigning values to parameters (*Treatments 处理*)
+     - **Randomization Unit (随机化单元)**: A pseudo-randomization process is applied to units (e.g., users or pages) to map them to variants (*Experiment units 实验对象*)
