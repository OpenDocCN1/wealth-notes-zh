# Sampling Distribution of p̂

+   - The sample proportion p̂ is the point estimator of the population proportion p.
+   - Recall that: p̂ = x / n
+     - x = the number of elements in the sample that possess the characteristic of interest
+   - p̂ is also a random variable.
+   - Its probability distribution is called the sampling distribution of p̂.
+   - Similarly, to determine how close the sample proportion p̂ is to the population proportion p, we need to understand the following:
+     1. The expected value or mean of p̂;
+     2. The standard deviation of p̂;
+     3. The shape or form of the sampling distribution of p̂.
