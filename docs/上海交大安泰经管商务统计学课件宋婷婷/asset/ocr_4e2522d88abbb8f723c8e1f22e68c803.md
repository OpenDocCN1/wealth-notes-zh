# Advantage of Factorials

+   - Suppose we have two factors A and B, each at two levels
+   - A one-factor-at-a-time design
+     - a total of 6 (3×2) observations are required to estimate the effects of 2 factors
+     - conclusion on interaction may be seriously in error
+   - A 2-factor factorial design
+     - a total of 4 observations are required
+     - effects of a factor can be estimated at several levels of other factors
+     - interaction can be studied
