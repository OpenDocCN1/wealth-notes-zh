# Motivations: Effluent Monitoring (污水发测)

+   - **Real problems:** Government wants to ensure that the effluent (处理过的污水) at a plant meets certain standards.
+     - In addition to the average PH level, the variance in the reported PH levels was also reviewed.

+   - **Based on the sample PH levels, we want to know:**
+     - What is the (estimated) variance of the PH levels?
+       - ⇒ Point estimator
+     - What is the 95% confidence interval of the variance?
+       - ⇒ Interval estimator
+     - Whether this plant has a variance of a properly functioning plant?
+       - ⇒ Hypothesis testing
