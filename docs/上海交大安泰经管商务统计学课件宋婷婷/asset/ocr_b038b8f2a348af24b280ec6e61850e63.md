# Motivating Example: Quality Control

+   - A factory purchased a new automated assembly line, and wants to monitor the quality of its manufacturing process.
+   - It is known that, when operating properly, the measure of output follows the normal distribution with mean equal to 12.
+   - The client wants to know: Is the process operating properly now?

+   - **Solution:** Take random samples to test whether the population mean is 12.
