# Example——蜜雪冰城

+   - Consider the production process of filling Ice Lemon Tea in mi xue bing cheng (蜜雪冰城).
+   - Real problem: We are interested in estimating the population variance for the Ice Lemon Tea filling process.
+   - Statistically: A sample of 20 containers is taken, and the sample variance for the filling quantities is found to be s² = 0.0025.
+   - Our interest will be in developing confidence interval with α = 0.05.
