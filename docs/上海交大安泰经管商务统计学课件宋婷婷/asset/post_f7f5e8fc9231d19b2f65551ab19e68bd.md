
## Interpretation of Interval Estimation: \(\sigma\) Known

- How to understand “confidence level”?
  - Any sample mean \(\bar{x}\) will provide an interval estimate for the population mean.
  - However, only the intervals constructed by the sample means within \(\mu \pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma_{\bar{x}}\) will include the population mean \(\mu\).
  - \(100(1-\alpha)\%\) of all the intervals constructed using \(\bar{x}\pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma_{\bar{x}}\) will contain the population mean.

![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_111_0.png)

## Example: CI with \(\sigma\)

- **Example:** 学生饭费
  - 已知某学校学生饭费的总体标准差是 \(\sigma=5\) 元，\(z_{0.025}=1.96\)。
  - 通常，随机抽样（\(n=36\)），得到学生饭费的样本均值 \(\bar{x}=20\) 元。
  - 采用前面的公式，我们就可以得到学生饭费均值的 95% 置信区间为
    \[
    20 \pm 1.96 \times \frac{5}{\sqrt{36}} = 20 \pm 1.63 \text{ 元}
    \]
- 思考：“有 95% 的概率认为以上区间会包含学生饭费的总体均值吗”？对吗？
- **Note:** True population mean is a fixed unknown value that is either inside or outside the CI with 100% certainty.

## Interval Estimate of Population Mean: \(\sigma\) Known

**Example:** SJTU 学生饭费 – True or False?

- A. 95% of the individuals in SJTU consume between $18.37 and $21.63.
- B. 95% of all sample means fall between $18.37 and $21.63.
- C. There is a 95% chance that the population mean falls between $18.37 and $21.63.
- D. If we repeat the computation and obtain a group of confidence intervals, then we would expect 95% of them will contain the population mean.

## Chi-square distribution

- **Definition:** \(\chi^2(k)\) is the sum of \(k\) squared independent standard normals.
  - If \(X_i \sim N(0,1)\), then \(X_i^2\) follows a chi-square distribution with degree of freedom 1, represented by \(X_i^2 \sim \chi^2(1)\).
  - If \(X_1^2, X_2^2, \ldots, X_k^2\) are independent \(\chi^2(1)\) variables, then \(\sum_{i=1}^{k} X_i^2\) follows a chi-square distribution with degree of freedom \(k\), represented by \(\sum_{i=1}^{k} X_i^2 \sim \chi^2(k)\).

![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_115_0.png)

## Student t-distribution

- **Definition:** If \(Y \sim N(0,1)\) and \(U \sim \chi^2(k)\), \(Y\) and \(U\) are independent, then
  \[
  t(k) = \frac{Y}{\sqrt{U/k}}
  \]
  follows a \(t\) distribution with degree of freedom \(k\).

## Interval Estimate of Population Mean: \(\sigma\) Unknown

- **Interval Estimation:** point estimator \(\pm \Delta\)
- **Key point:** What is \(\Delta\) when \(\sigma\) is unknown?
  - When \(\sigma\) is known, we choose \(\Delta = z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt{n}\) so that there is \(1-\alpha\) probability that the error \(\bar{x}-\mu\) is within \(\pm\Delta\), i.e.,
    \[
    P\{-z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt{n} < \bar{x}-\mu < z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt{n}\} = 1-\alpha
    \]
  - Now we only know sample standard deviation, \(s\).
  - How to define \(\Delta\)? \(\Rightarrow\) The key is to find the distribution of
    \[
    t = \frac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}
    \]

## Understand \(t = \frac{\bar{x} - \mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}\)

We decompose \(t = \frac{\bar{x} - \mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}\) as follows:

\[
t = \frac{\frac{\bar{x}-\mu}{\sqrt{\sigma^2/n}}}{\sqrt{\frac{s^2(n-1)/\sigma^2}{n-1}}}
  = \frac{N(0,1)}{\sqrt{\chi^2(n-1)/(n-1)}}
\]

- Assumption: Let each element \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\) be \(N(\mu, \sigma^2)\) r.v.
- Numerator: \(\frac{\bar{x}-\mu}{\sqrt{\sigma^2/n}} \sim N(0,1)\)
- Denominator: \(\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma^2} \sim \chi^2(n-1)\)
- \(\bar{x}\) and \(s\) are independent (Why?)

## Construction of CI: \(\sigma\) Unknown

- **Interval Estimation:** point estimator \(\pm \Delta\)
- **Key point:** What is \(\Delta\) when \(\sigma\) is unknown?
- When \(\sigma\) is unknown, we choose \(\Delta = t_{\alpha/2,n-1}s/\sqrt{n}\) so that there is \(1-\alpha\) probability that the error \(\bar{x}-\mu\) is within \(\pm\Delta\), i.e.,
  \[
  P\{-t_{\alpha/2,n-1}s/\sqrt{n} < \bar{x}-\mu < t_{\alpha/2,n-1}s/\sqrt{n}\} = 1-\alpha
  \]
- Now we use sample standard deviation, \(s\).
- \(t_{\alpha/2}\) is the \(t\) value providing an area of \(\alpha/2\) in the upper tail of the \(t\) distribution with \(n-1\) degrees of freedom.

## Example: CI without \(\sigma\)

- **Example:** 学生饭费
  - 由于我们不知道学生饭费的总体标准差，所以若我们采用 \(\sigma\) 未知时总体均值的估计方法。
  - 通常，随机抽样（\(n=36\)），得到学生饭费的样本均值 \(\bar{x}=20\) 元，样本标准差 \(s=6\) 元。
  - 此时自由度为 35，\(t_{0.025}=2.03\)。
  - 采用前面的公式，我们就可以得到学生饭费均值的 95% 置信区间为
    \[
    20 \pm 2.03 \times \frac{6}{\sqrt{36}} = 20 \pm 2.03 \text{ 元}
    \]
