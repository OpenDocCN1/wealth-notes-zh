# Formulation of Hypotheses

+   General formulation of Null and Alternative Hypotheses
+   
+   $H_0: \theta \in \Theta_0 \quad \text{versus} \quad H_a: \theta \in \Theta_0^c,$
+   
+   where $\theta$ is the parameter to test, and $\Theta_0$ is a closed set.

+   Examples: the typical tests for the population mean $\theta \equiv \mu$

+   Two-tailed test: $\Theta_0 = \{\mu_0\}$
+   
+   $H_0: \mu = \mu_0, \quad \text{versus} \quad H_a: \mu \neq \mu_0.$

+   Upper-tailed test: $\Theta_0 = (-\infty, \mu_0]$ 
+   
+   $H_0: \mu \le \mu_0, \quad \text{versus} \quad H_a: \mu > \mu_0.$

+   Lower-tailed test: $\Theta_0 = [\mu_0, \infty)$
+   
+   $H_0: \mu \ge \mu_0, \quad \text{versus} \quad H_a: \mu < \mu_0.$
