
## Motivations: Effluent Monitoring (污水监测)

- **Real problems:** Government wants to ensure that the effluent (处理过的污水) at a plant meets certain standards.
  - In addition to the average pH level, the variance in the reported pH levels was also reviewed.
- **Based on the sample pH levels, we want to know:**
  - What is the (estimated) variance of the pH levels?
    - ⇒ Point estimator
  - What is the 95% confidence interval of the variance?
    - ⇒ Interval estimator
  - Whether this plant has a variance of a properly functioning plant?
    - ⇒ Hypothesis testing

## Interval Estimation of Variance and Standard Deviation

- Denote \(\chi^2_{(1-\alpha)}\) to be the \(\alpha\)-quantile of the chi-square distribution

- Then there is a \((1-\alpha)\) probability such that

\[
\chi^2_{(1-\alpha/2)} \leq \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma^2} \leq \chi^2_{\alpha/2}
\]

![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_203_0.png)

- Performing algebraic manipulation, we get **interval estimator** for variance

\[
\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{\alpha/2}} \leq \sigma^2 \leq \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{(1-\alpha/2)}}
\]

- Taking square root, we get **interval estimator** for standard deviation

\[
\sqrt{\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{\alpha/2}}} \leq \sigma \leq \sqrt{\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{(1-\alpha/2)}}}
\]

## Example——蜜雪冰城

![](img/ea9cdfad8dc7821ead4e68fc7aaa5ca3_205_0.png)

- 背景：如果要论2021登上半壁江山的饮品，蜜雪冰城主题曲绝对算得上其中之一。
- 于1997年创立的蜜雪冰城大概也没有想到，卖了20多年茶，后来却是凭借在网络上被广泛传播的旋律而彻底火了。
- 由于在主题曲大火后不久，蜜雪冰城却被曝出食品安全问题，蜜雪冰城官方也为此作出整改并道歉。

- Consider the production process of filling Ice Lemon Tea in mi xue bing cheng (蜜雪冰城).
- **Real problem:** We are interested in estimating the population variance for the Ice Lemon Tea filling process.
- **Statistically:** A sample of 20 containers is taken, and the sample variance for the filling quantities is found to be $s^2 = 0.0025$.
- Our interest will be in developing confidence interval with $\alpha = 0.05$.
- Since $\chi^2_{0.975}(19) = 8.907$ and $\chi^2_{0.025}(19) = 32.852$, the C.I. is

$$
\frac{(19)(0.0025)}{32.852} \le \sigma^2 \le \frac{(19)(0.0025)}{8.907},
$$

i.e.,

$$
0.0014 \le \sigma^2 \le 0.0053
$$

$$
0.0380 \le \sigma \le 0.0730
$$

## Hypothesis Testing About a Population Variance

- Three forms for a hypothesis test about a population variance:
  - Two-tailed test: $H_0: \sigma^2 = \sigma_0^2$, versus $H_a: \sigma^2 \neq \sigma_0^2$
  - Upper-tailed test: $H_0: \sigma^2 \leq \sigma_0^2$, versus $H_a: \sigma^2 > \sigma_0^2$
  - Lower-tailed test: $H_0: \sigma^2 \geq \sigma_0^2$, versus $H_a: \sigma^2 < \sigma_0^2$

- Step 1: state the null and alternative hypotheses
  - $H_0: \sigma^2 \le \sigma_0^2$; $H_a: \sigma^2 > \sigma_0^2$.
- Step 2: select the level of significance $\alpha$.
- Step 3: the test statistic is specified by

$$
\chi^2 = \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma_0^2}.
$$

When $H_0$ is true with equality holds, it follows $\chi^2(n-1)$.

- Step 4: Two approaches to determine the rejection rule
  - Critical-value approach
  - p-value approach
