# Motivations: Testing for Independence

+   - Independence: Two variables are independent or Not?
+   - Example: Parents income ⇒ Children Education
+     - A survey is conducted of 175 young adults on their highest level of schooling (university, high school or neither).
+     - Their parents are classified as wealthy, middle class or poor
+   - Sample data: the results are summarized as follows

| Parents | Level of Schooling |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|
|  | University | High School | None | Total |
| Wealthy | 20 | 15 | 10 | 45 |
| Middle Class | 40 | 25 | 20 | 85 |
| Poor | 8 | 14 | 23 | 45 |
| Total | 68 | 54 | 53 | 175 |

+   - Question: Is the person’s level of schooling independent of their parents’ wealth?
