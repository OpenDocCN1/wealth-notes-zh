## Multinomial Distribution

- Multinomial distribution is a direct extension of the binomial distribution.
- Consider a series of *n* independent trials. At each trial, one and only one of *k* outcomes can occur, with corresponding probability $p_1, \ldots, p_k$. $(\sum_{i=1}^{k} p_i = 1)$
- Let $X_i$ = number of times that outcome *i* occurs, $i = 1, \ldots, k$. Then the vector

  $$(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_k)$$

  follows a multinomial distribution.
- Properties
  - $\sum_{i=1}^{k} X_i = n$
  - $X_i$ has a binomial distribution

## Goodness of Fit Test: Multinomial Distribution

- State the null and alternative hypotheses.
  - $H_0$: The population is NOT different from a multinomial distribution with specified probabilities for each of the $k$ categories
  - $H_a$: The population is different from a multinomial distribution with specified probabilities for each of the $k$ categories
- Select a random sample and record the observed frequency $f_i$ for each of the $k$ categories.
- Assuming $H_0$ is true, compute the expected frequency $e_i$ in each category by multiplying the category probability by the sample size.
- Compute the value of the test statistic.

$$\chi^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \frac{(f_i - e_i)^2}{e_i}$$

Note: The test statistic has a chi-square distribution with $k - 1$ df provided that the expected frequencies are 5 or more for all categories.

- Rejection rule:
  - p-value approach: Reject $H_0$ if p-value ≤ $\alpha$
  - Critical value approach: Reject $H_0$ if $\chi^2 > \chi^2_{\alpha, k-1}$

## Example: Market Share Research

- Hypotheses

  $H_0$: $p_A = 0.30$, $p_B = 0.50$, and $p_C = 0.20$
  $H_a$: Not $p_A = 0.30$, $p_B = 0.50$, and $p_C = 0.20$

  where

  $p_A$ = probability a customer purchases the company A product
  $p_B$ = probability a customer purchases the company B product
  $p_C$ = probability a customer purchases the company C product

- Expected Frequencies

  $e_1 = 200 \times p_A = 60$, $e_2 = 200 \times p_B = 100$, $e_3 = 200 \times p_C = 40$

- Test Statistic

  $\chi^2 = (48 - 60)^2 / 60 + (98 - 100)^2 / 100 + (54 - 40)^2 / 40 = 7.34$

- Rejection Rule ($\alpha = 0.05$, d.f. = 2)
  - p-Value Approach: p-value = 0.0255 < $\alpha$
  - Critical Value Approach: $\chi^2 > \chi^2_{0.05,2} = 5.991$
- Conclusion: The introduction of a new Company C product will alter the market shares for the three companies.
