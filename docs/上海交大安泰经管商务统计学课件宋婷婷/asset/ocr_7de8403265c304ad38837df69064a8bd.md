# ANOVA: Motivation Models

+   - **The Goal:** to compare means among different groups. (treatments)
+   - **ANOVA** seeks to achieve the above goal by identifying sources of variation in a numerical dependent variable $X = \{x_{ij}\}$.
+     - $j$ denotes the group the subject belongs to from $1, \dots, k$
+     - $i$ denotes the $i$th sample in the group from $1, \dots, n_j$
+   - We consider a simple model for $x_{ij}$
+     - Model 1 (under $H_0$): $x_{ij} = \mu + \varepsilon_{ij}$
+     - Model 2 (under $H_a$): $x_{ij} = \mu + \alpha_j + \varepsilon_{ij}$
+   - Intuitively, the variation in $X$ under Model 1 ONLY comes from the error term $\varepsilon_{ij}$.
+   - But the variation in $X$ under Model 2 comes from two sources: treatment adjustments $\alpha_j$ and the error term $\varepsilon_{ij}$.
