# Simple Random Sampling

+   - We may start the selection of random numbers anywhere in the table and move systematically in a direction of our choice:
+     - For example, we start from 9986 (i.e., the last four digits of the second number). It is greater than 9000 and is discarded.
+   - Then, the following four digits (i.e., 7174) are smaller than 9000 and thus are selected.

![](bbox=[0.32, 0.42, 0.68, 0.78])

+   - Note: When we refer to simple random sampling, we will assume the sampling is without replacement.
