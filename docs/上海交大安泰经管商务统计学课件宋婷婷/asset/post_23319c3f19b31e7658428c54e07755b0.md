
## Sampling Distribution of \bar{x}

- **Form of the Sampling Distribution of \bar{x}**
  1. The population has a normal distribution
     - The sampling distribution of \bar{x} is normally distributed for any sample size.
  2. The population does not have a normal distribution
     - We need the sample size to be large, then the sampling distribution of \bar{x} is approximately normal (Central Limit Theorem).

## Sampling Distribution of x̄

- The practical value of sampling distribution for x̄
  - We cannot expect the sample mean x̄ to exactly equal the population mean μ.
  - Given the sampling distribution, we can provide probability information about the gap between x̄ and μ.

- Example: 纸. 组织(计算误差在0.5分以内的概率)
  - Given that μ=6, σ=1 and sample size n is 16.
  - E(x̄)=μ=6; σ_x̄ = σ/√n =0.25

Pr{5.5 ≤ x̄ ≤ 6.5}
= Pr{ (5.5 − E(x̄))/σ_x̄ ≤ (x̄ − E(x̄))/σ_x̄ ≤ (6.5 − E(x̄))/σ_x̄ }
≈ Φ(2) − Φ(−2) = 0.9544

- The probability that |x̄ − μ| ≤ 0.5 is 95.44%

## Sampling Distribution of p̂

- The sample proportion p̂ is the point estimator of the population proportion p.
- Recall that: p̂ = x / n
  - x = the number of elements in the sample that possess the characteristic of interest
- p̂ is also a random variable.
- Its probability distribution is called the sampling distribution of p̂.
- Similarly, to determine how close the sample proportion p̂ is to the population proportion p, we need to understand the following:
  1. The expected value or mean of p̂;
  2. The standard deviation of p̂;
  3. The shape or form of the sampling distribution of p̂.

## Sampling Distribution of $\bar{p}$

- The population distribution is **Bernoulli distribution**. So the sampling distribution of $\bar{p}$ is not exact normal.

- Recall that:

  - Suppose we randomly pick up $n$ i.i.d. students. Here $X_i = 1$ indicates a female student and $X_i = 0$ indicates a male student, i.e.,

$X_i \sim \operatorname{Bernoulli}(p),\ \text{for } i = 1, 2, \ldots, n.$

$P(X = 1) = p$

$P(X = 0) = 1 - p$

$\mu \equiv E(X) = p$

$\sigma^2 \equiv Var(X) = E(X^2) - E(X)^2 = p(1-p)$

## Sampling Distribution of $\bar{p}$

- **The Practical Value of sampling distribution for $\bar{p}$**
  - As similar to $\bar{x}$, given the sampling distribution, we can provide probability information about the gap between $\bar{p}$ and $p$.
  - **Example:** 统 组（假设对这 组，给定 25 个 6 的人数占主打人数的比率 $p=40\%$，随机为抽取 25 位学生的组织算到地计值 $\bar{p}$，计算 $\bar{p}$ 的误差在 0.05 以内的概率）
    - Given that $p=0.4$, sample size $n$ is 25.
    - $E(\bar{p})=p=0.4$; $\sigma_{\bar{p}}=\sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n}}=0.098$
    - $Pr\{0.35\leq\bar{p}\leq0.45\}=Pr\left\{\frac{0.35-E(\bar{p})}{\sigma_{\bar{p}}}\leq\frac{\bar{p}-E(\bar{p})}{\sigma_{\bar{p}}}\leq\frac{0.45-E(\bar{p})}{\sigma_{\bar{p}}}\right\}$
    - $\approx\Phi(0.51)-\Phi(-0.51)=0.39$
    - The probability that $|\bar{p}-p|\leq0.05$ is only 39%
  - Similarly, the **larger sample size** provides a **higher probability** that the sample proportion is within a specified distance of the population proportion.
