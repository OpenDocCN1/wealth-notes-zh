# The 12 Steps of AA

AA's 12-Step approach follows a set of guidelines designed as "steps" toward recovery, and members can revisit these steps at any time. The 12 Steps are:⁹

+   1. (View Step 1) We admitted we were powerless over alcohol—that our lives had become unmanageable.

+   2. (View Step 2) Came to believe that a Power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity.

+   3. (View Step 3) Made a decision to

最有意思是前两个步骤：

+   ① 承认我们自己意志力很薄弱，抵抗不了酒精的诱惑，所以我们的生活才会变得一团糟。

+   ② 相信世界上存在一股比我们自身意志力还要强大的力量，能让我们恢复理智和清醒。
