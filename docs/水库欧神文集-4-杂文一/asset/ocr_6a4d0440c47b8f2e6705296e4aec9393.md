我们抹去“股神”“股霸”“天才恶庄”之类神话，从原汁原味的本意出发，证券交易所所允许存在的原因：

证券交易所在各国大义的名分，股市可以优化资源配置。

但是 we look at the Chinese stock market. It does not actually conform to the 'optimization of resource allocation' at all. (Translation: 但是我们看回中国股市。中国股市，其实并不符合“优化资源配置”的。)

As for the Chinese stock market, those who are most likely to gain 'funding' and 'capital increase allocation' are never the private enterprises with the most competitive vitality. (Translation: 对于中国股市而言，最能够获得“融资”，最能够获得“增发配股”的。从来不是最有竞争活力的民营企业。)

The basic positioning of the Chinese stock market is to finance, infuse, and rescue state-owned enterprises. (Translation: 中国股市，基本定位，就是为国企融资，输血，解困。)

Over the years, the stock market has been a money-swallowing black hole, consuming countless shareholders' funds. Shareholders have not obtained the 'legendary returns' either; for every ten stocks, nine lose money. (Translation: 历年来，股市象一个吸钱的黑洞，吞噬了无数股民资金。股民们，也并没有获得 NYSE “传说中的回报”，十股九亏。)

In terms of 'optimization of resource allocation', the stock market's contribution rate to the GDP of all of China is a negative figure. (Translation: 从“优化资源配置”而言，股市对于全中国的 GDP 贡献率是一个负数。)

If you completely shut down the stock market, national growth might actually be faster. (Translation: 如果你彻底关掉股市，国力增长，可能还会更快些。)

+   *   The contribution of securities firms to GDP is positive.
*   The contribution of the overall stock market to GDP is negative.
