# The Midas Muffler Man
And Our Unforgettable
Fishing Trip

> "He is just another repairman," we thought. But he wasn't. He was something very different and he treated us in service we'll never forget.

By Tex McCray and Jinx (Falkenburg) McCray
TV and Radio's Famous "Mr. & Mrs." Team

![](bbox=[0.44, 0.34, 0.71, 0.48])

A... rising car! Jinx, the boys and I were on our way at last. The car was purring like a kitten and as we drew closer to our favorite spot we could almost feel the tug of a fish on our line. Then it happened. Our muffler blew out and our car began to roar. What is a muffler after all? It doesn't make the car go and it doesn't make it stop, but when you need one you usually need it bad. The noise was unbearable. "There goes today's fishing," the boys moaned. "Oh, no." I bravely said but secretly I feared they were right. We saw ourselves laid up for hours in a strange place and at the mercy of an unknown repairman. On the outskirts of the next town we saw his sign. We had heard of Midas Muffler Shops before so we gave it a try. Kevin was eyeing his brand new fishing tackle glumly when the Midas man walked over. "Relax, folks," he said. "It will only take 15 minutes." And so it did. Jinx settled in the comfortable waiting room while the boys and I, always looking for a chance to peek under our car, went to watch the Midas "team" at work.
