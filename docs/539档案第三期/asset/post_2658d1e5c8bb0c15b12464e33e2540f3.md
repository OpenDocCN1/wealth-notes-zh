# 和我们难忘的垂钓之旅

> “他只是一个普通的维修工，”我们想。但是我们错了。他和别的维修工不同，他的服务，让我们终生难忘。

来自：泰克斯·麦凯瑞、金克斯(法肯伯格)·麦凯瑞
著名电视台及电台主持人 “先生&太太” 组合

垂钓之旅！我带着太太金克斯，还有孩子们，终于出发了。汽车像只小猫一样，呼噜噜作响，当我们离最喜欢的地方越来越近时，我们几乎可以感受到鱼儿向我们招手。

然后，问题出现了。我们的消音器烧断了，汽车开始轰鸣。消音器是什么？它不能给汽车提供行驶的动力，也不能让汽车停止。但是，一旦它出现了问题，你却会迫切地需要它。没有它，汽车发出的噪音让人难以忍受。

“今天钓鱼泡汤了，”孩子们抱怨道。“哦，不会的，”我强颜欢笑地说道，但我心里却担心他们是对的。我们仿佛看到自己躺在一个陌生的地方几个小时，看着某个陌生的修理工修理我们的消音器。

在汽车行驶到下一个小镇的郊外时，我们远远地看到了麦得斯消音器店。之前我们听说过这个店，所以我们决定试一试。

当麦德斯维修工走过我们身边时，凯文郁闷的盯着他的崭新的渔具。“放松，伙计们，”他说。“15分钟就可以搞定了。”

果然，整个过程只用了15分钟。

金克斯舒适的坐在等候室，而我和孩子们则不停地张望，悄悄地看着我们的车，看着忙碌中的麦德斯“团队”。当看到我们之前那个生锈的，冒着烟的老消音器，被一个崭新的取代时，我们内心真的很高兴。最后，我们终于听从了麦德斯维修工的建议，放松了下来。

“我认为你们15分钟肯定修不好，”我坦诚地说道。“没想到你们的速度这么快？”

“哦，这很简单，”麦德斯维修工说道。“我们每天的工作就是做这个。对于我们来说，速度是工作的乐趣之一，我们一直追求精益求精。”

“但是，你们怎么只专注于消音器呢？”

“很多人都问过我们这个问题，”他笑道。“其实，需要消音器却不知道去哪里找的人，比你想象的多得多。我们以更好的方式满足车主的这个需求。这就是为什么我们能够在全国范围内提供这种专业服务。”

我们又要出发了。汽车不再嗡嗡作响了。

但是，当我看到账单时，我又得到了一个惊喜……

# The invisible
plastic-glass sandwich
that rides between
you and danger

by John Hayes

Incredible as it may seem in this automotive age, fewer than 1 in 20 car owners know whether or not there is a plastic safety layer in the windshield and side windows of their automobiles. Many may know only that their automobile has some kind of “safety glass”—but there is a lot more to the story.

The “Plastic-Glass Sandwich:” is formed by placing a sheet of vinyl butyral, a tough adhesive plastic, between two layers of glass (see sketch above). Then all three are permanently laminated into one unit.

Thanks to this plastic safety layer, laminated safety glass reduces the hazard of shattering glass. And the optical qualities of this plastic-glass sandwich, when plate glass is used, are superior to any other type of automotive safety glass. In addition, laminated safety glass is easily cut to any size by glass installers.

Because it meets the optical and safety specifications set up by the American Standards Assoc., laminated safety glass with the plastic safety layer has for years been the only material used in windshields of all motor vehicles produced in the U.S.A. Most of them use laminated safety glass in side windows also.

Laminated safety glass is performing in many fields outside of transportation—for the plastic layer that provides safety can be printed in beautiful colors and designs. It can be a shatterproof shower stall, a table or dresser top, a beautiful room divider. As for the future—a visit to the Monsanto “House of the Future” in Disneyland will give you a hint of the many wonderful ways this “Plastic-Glass Sandwich” will contribute to your future living.

![](img/4a4110a48dd9dbd39e3d48b14a410300_157_1.png)

How can you tell if you have the plastic layer of safety around you as you ride? You know it must be in the windshield—but what about the door windows? If you have laminated safety glass, you can readily see the edge of the plastic safety layer by examining the door vents, tops of door windows, or other exposed edges of the glass.

The Monsanto Chemical Company, Plastics Division, supplies plastic used by America’s leading glass companies for the plastic safety layer in making laminated safety glass.
