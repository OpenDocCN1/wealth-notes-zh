Solves Blistering Problem – For a long time blistering has been home owners' most serious paint problem. Until now, it has been an almost insoluble, recurring predicament.

Blistering is caused by moisture which often is generated in surprisingly large quantities every day inside the average modern home (by appliances, showers, cooking, etc.). Paint blistering results when such moisture vapor tries to escape through the walls and gets trapped by old-style paint films which cannot "breathe." (The better you've insulated and weather-stripped your house, the harder it is for this moisture to get out any other way.) The combination of the new Du Pont Primer and "Lucite" House Paint can give you protection against blistering because it is chemically engineered to allow moisture vapor to pass through while shutting out water penetration from the outside.
