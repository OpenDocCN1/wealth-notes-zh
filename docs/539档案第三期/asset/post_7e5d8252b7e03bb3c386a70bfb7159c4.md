## 30、每个人只有两只
### Only two to a customer.

![](img/4a4110a48dd9dbd39e3d48b14a410300_128_0.png)

Ever think about the old saying: "An eye for an eye"? It comes from a code of justice developed over 5,000 years ago. A time when the value of an eye was equal to the value of life itself.

Are our eyes any less precious today? We still depend on them, as on no other senses, every waking moment of our lives. We still employ them as the basis for our entire system of teaching and learning.

Yet there is a difference between then and now. A tremendous difference. Now we ask our eyes to work as they were never intended to.

Once we used our eyes to sight game at a distance. Or to spot an enemy in time to welcome him properly. Now we spend hours in reading, watching TV, sewing, operating machinery. All close-up viewing.

Once we drove horses and buggies. And this activity called for normal distance viewing. Now we drive cars. At high speeds. Speeds demanding lightning-fast adjustments in eye focusing. Imposing enormous tension, strain, energy output.

Once we used our eyes almost entirely for long-range daylight seeing. Now we use (and abuse) them for long periods in close-up work under artificial light.

All this, mind you, we require of our one pair of eyes. What’s really incredible is that our eyes perform the wonders they do as long as they do.

Fortunately, help is available when we require it. Modern ophthalmic science and techniques now provide an amazing range of visual aids. We can get lenses that are anti-reflection coated. Lenses that are impact resistant. Tri-focals. Prescription sunglasses. Lenses and glasses custom-designed for the special ways we now work, study and play.

Whether you wear glasses or not, don’t forget a very important thing. Your eyes are changing. Gradually, perhaps imperceptibly. So that you can actually be unaware of the fact that you’re not seeing as well as you did. Or as you might.

How do you find out what the change, if any, is? Simply by having your eyes examined regularly, once a year, by a qualified eye-care practitioner. Makes sense, doesn’t it?

And above all, remember this: only two to a customer. Treat them accordingly. Better Vision Institute.

## 每个人只有两只

![](img/4a4110a48dd9dbd39e3d48b14a410300_129_0.png)

你有没有认真想过一句古话，“以眼还眼”？这是5000多年前，一只眼睛和生命有着同等价值的时代，某法典里的一句话。

难道今天，我们的眼睛没有以前珍贵了吗？不是的，现在我们对眼睛的依赖程度，依然大于其他感官，醒着的每一刻，我们都要依赖它。我们仍然把它当做我们整个教学和学习系统的基础。

然而，现在和以前不太一样。实际上有很大的不同。现在，我们对眼睛的要求，远高于以前。

以前，我们用眼睛观看远处的游戏。或观测远处的敌人，一旦发现敌情，快速应对。而现在，我们花数小时阅读、看电视、缝纫、操作机械。都是近距离观看。

以前，我们驾驶马车，骑马。这种是正常距离的观看。而现在，我们开车。高速行驶。驾驶速度，要求我们的眼睛以闪电般的速度调整。我们的眼睛高度紧张，接受着巨大的压力。

以前，我们几乎只在白天的正常光线下用眼。而现在，我们长时间在灯光下近距离用眼。

这一切，请注意，都说明我们需要我们的双眼。真正令人难以置信的是，我们的眼睛一直创造着奇迹。

幸运的是，现在只要我们的双眼需要，我们就能获得相应的帮助。现代护眼的科学和技术，给我们提供了一系列了不起的视觉设备。有抗辐射眼镜，抗撞击眼镜，多焦点眼镜。还有针对我们现在的生活，学习，玩耍时不同需求而研发的眼镜。

不管你是否佩戴眼镜，都不要忘记一件重要的事实。你的眼睛在变化。渐渐地，也许根本不会察觉。所以，你的视觉没有以前那么好了，你可能也察觉不到。

那么，如果你的眼睛起了变化，你怎么才能察觉呢？只有定期去专业的眼科检查眼睛，一年一次吧。这很重要，不是吗？

总之，记住：一个人只有两只眼睛。请珍惜。视更佳研究所。

## 32、“长途直拨”的神奇世界

### The magic world of "DDD"

The late morning sun traced its bright yellow patterns on the kitchen floor as a young New York City housewife reached for the telephone to call her mother in San Francisco. As she flicked the dial an ingenious nation-wide "machine" sprang into action. A few seconds later came her mother's cheery "hello."

Friendly voices like these are getting through faster today, thanks to a wonderful new system of long distance telephony: Direct Distance Dialing, or DDD. This remarkable system lets you call a telephone in a far-off city without an operator's help as easily as you can call a neighbor across the street. Today, over 5 million telephone users in some 400 communities can dial directly 35 million distant telephones.

Behind this remarkable system is a maze of intricate telephone equipment – including some of the most complex devices ever built by man. And all of this equipment is made by Western Electric, manufacturing and supply unit of the Bell System.

Our job today is to produce large quantities of this complex telephone equipment. Like the lightning fast switching units and the electronic devices that determine the routing of each call as it goes through... making sure a call will choose the most direct path available as it threads its way through automatic switching centers across the country. Or the mechanical bookkeeping system that automatically produces a complete record of the call on perforated tape... permanently recording who made the call, who was called, how long it lasted.

When will we all enjoy DDD? By the end of next year more than two-thirds of all Bell System telephones from coast to coast will be within dialing distance of the nearly 8 million subscribers served by DDD. And by 1965 it is expected that every dial telephone in the Bell System will be linked to the DDD network. Meanwhile, Western Electric will be engineering, manufacturing and installing new equipment in dial switching centers... to help put all of America at your fingertips.

A big job? Yes, and it's a result of the way we work with the Bell Telephone Laboratories who design the equipment and the people of your local Bell telephone company who operate it—working together to bring you the latest advances in telephone science. That's how we at Western Electric help give you continually improving, ever more convenient telephone service.
