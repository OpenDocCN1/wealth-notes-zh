# 39、一个适合发展的小镇

# A good town to GROW in

![](bbox=[0.54, 0.275, 0.732, 0.36])

Austin, Minnesota, is a typical Main Street town of 24,000 people, some of whom can still recall the day when George Hormel and his friend, George Peterson, opened a butcher shop in 1891. Today more than 4,500 Austin folks work in the big Hormel packing plant across the Cedar River, home of Spam, Dinty Moore and Mary Kitchen foods, Hormel Hams and Chili and 693 other nationally known meat products.

Like many industries that grew up in the warm and friendly climate of a true "Home Town," the Hormel Company’s affection for Austin is genuine. Hormel men in Texas, Nebraska, California and Massachusetts think of Austin as home and take special pride in Austin’s progress and achievements.

Geo. A. Hormel & Company believes that the unique flavor and character of Austin deserve to be brought to the attention of other industries and other manufacturers

looking for the ideal Midwest "Home Town" where they can put down roots and grow as Hormel has.

This, then, is Austin: High-quality people. Productive and understanding labor. Underlying spring water. Sewage facilities with surplus capacity for many years. On the new Inter-State Highway, coast-to-coast. Adequate rail transportation. One of the nation's best school systems, plus an area vocational school to direct training to any need. Moisture average, 29 inches a year. Farm prosperity is high, stable, diversified and independent.

If you want to know more about Austin, its people and its opportunities, please write to me or to the Austin Industrial Development Corporation. It's my home town, and I hope I can help to make it yours.

H. H. Corey, Chairman of the Board
Geo. A. Hormel & Co., Austin, Minn.
