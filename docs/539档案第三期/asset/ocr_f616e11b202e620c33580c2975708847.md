Until now, all bras were made to fit an “average” woman ... turned out very much as if all women were identical, and their figures never ever changed.

But the truth is, most women aren’t average. In the first place, nature being fickle, their breasts aren’t exactly the same size. Also, they have periodic weight changes—often accompanied by a bust change of a full size or more. So no wonder it’s difficult to find a bra that really fits all the time.
