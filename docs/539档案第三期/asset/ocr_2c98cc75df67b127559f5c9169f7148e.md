The new thinking about dieting says don't turn your normal eating habits upside down. Don't involve yourself in bizarre "wonder" diets that are in conflict with sound nutritional principles. Go ahead and eat the good things you have been accustomed to having on your table. Cut the portions down, but don't out them out.

With this new and sensible approach, sugar has been positioned as today's big news in dieting, because sugar can be a most important aid in controlling weight.
