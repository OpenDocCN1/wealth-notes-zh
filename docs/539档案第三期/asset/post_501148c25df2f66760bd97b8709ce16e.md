## 24、6-8岁儿童的神奇世界

### The Wonderful World ... of your 6 to 8-year-old child

![](img/4a4110a48dd9dbd39e3d48b14a410300_105_0.png)

It's a busy, wonderful world for children aged 6 to 8... and it will become more so as they "graduate" from home and begin to live on their own as primary-graders.

The wider world which the young child enters isn't always a smooth one... and the guidance, sympathy and reassurance of "moms" and "dads" can be as vital as when your child was only a toddler.

Those early years at school, for instance, are often difficult. A child may become shy, timid, or worried. Such behavior is a signal for reassurance... rather than pushing or prodding. With the right support at home and school, youngsters usually make a good adjustment and regain in courage and confidence.

It's most important for parents to show enthusiastic interest in a youngster's school work. Take time to listen, to praise and to encourage—children thrive on it.

Even though your youngster may be the picture of health, don't neglect regular medical and dental check-ups. Be sure to ask your child's physician about any "booster" shots that may be needed to renew protection against polio, tetanus, diphtheria and other diseases.

Since your youngster will be away from you more and more, re-emphasize the importance of safety. Be sure to show your child where he should cross the street, and emphasize crossing only with the green light.

Should your youngster have special difficulties with school work, an examination should be made of the eyes and ears. Defects which can be corrected are often the cause of low grades and ultimate dislike of school.

## 25、不管你开的什么车,这里的方法都能让你更加安全地驾驶!

![](img/4a4110a48dd9dbd39e3d48b14a410300_109_0.png)

### Whatever you drive, here's how to drive it more safely!

Every time you take to the road you have a great responsibility. Only when you drive with care, caution and courtesy, at all times, can our roads become safer for all. Here are some safety suggestions:

- ✓ Check your speed—About 3 out of 10 drivers involved in fatal accidents each year are guilty of violating speed laws. Always slow down at night and when road, traffic and weather conditions are hazardous.

- ✓ Check yourself—About 1 out of 14 drivers involved in fatal accidents has a physical or mental condition—such as worry, fatigue and sleepiness—that is a contributing factor in the accident. Never drive when upset or tired.

- ✓ Check your car—Keeping your car in safe operating condition is your responsibility—not your mechanic's. You can judge for yourself whether brakes, tires, steering wheel, lights and windshield wipers are in proper working order. If you notice any defects, have them corrected immediately. Don't wait until your next semiannual car check to have even the most minor trouble corrected.

- ✓ Check your driving habits—Now and then, the most skillful drivers tend to become a bit careless. They may become less considerate of other drivers and of pedestrians—or take chances on violating this or that traffic law. Remember, all rules of the road are made to help you, not to hinder you. When you violate a law, you are asking for an accident.

So, always drive as if your life depended on it. It does!
