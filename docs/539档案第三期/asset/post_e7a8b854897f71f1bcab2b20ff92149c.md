# Your child—and
the psychology of variety

ONE OF THE things that 50 years of making cereals has taught us is never to be amazed at the many good things that come from the good grains. But we were caught a bit off guard recently when psychologists pointed out that one of our products is a character-maker.

They were referring to our Variety Pack. This, as you may know, is an assortment of ten individual boxes of our most popular cereals. We have come to look at it as a very convenient way for everyone in the family to get his choice. For it contains cereals that are flaked, puffed, popped and shredded—of corn, wheat, rice—ready-sweetened and not—all the variety anyone could ask for.

But our psychologist friends pointed out that there’s something else in our Variety Pack. You can’t see it, but it’s good for you just the same. It is—“an opportunity for character-building freedom of expression in arriving at and making your own decisions.”

![](img/4a4110a48dd9dbd39e3d48b14a410300_69_0.png)

What does that mean? (We asked, too!) It simply means that being able to make up their own minds—even on little things—helps shape youngsters’ characters. Gives ’em confidence, helps ’em make decisions. Helps ’em grow up mentally, while they’re growing physically. Helps free ’em from frustrations of always being told what and what not to do.

We not only thought this made a lot of sense, but was interesting enough to pass along to you.

Not that we expect all youngsters who get to make their own decisions from Kellogg’s Variety Pack will automatically grow up to be chairmen of boards of directors! But isn’t it nice to know, that you are also serving up a little lesson in life when you put Kellogg’s Variety Pack in front of your child at breakfast. Why—even that big boy at your house may find it tastes extra good to be a man of decision!

# 你的孩子——不同的心理

从 50 年的谷物制作经验中，我们学到一点，那就是好的谷物会带给你许多意想不到的收获。

但是，我们最近真的被一件事震惊了。心理学家指出，我们的一款产品能塑造性格。

他们说的是我们的家乐氏谷物套装。你可能知道，这款套装中包含了 10 种独立包装的最受欢迎的谷物。我们最初的想法，只是希望能给大家提供方便，让家中每个人都能享用到自己喜欢的谷物。因为，这里包含了薄片型，膨化型，细片型的玉米，小麦，大米——有含糖的，有不含糖的——它的多样性能够满足每个人的需求。

但是，我们的心理学家，也是我们的朋友，指出这种套装还有一种好处。你看不到，但是却真实存在的好处。那就是——“可以帮助塑造自由表达愿望，以及自己做决定的性格。”

为什么这么说呢？（这也是我们的疑问！）

也就是说，自己做决定——即使是在很小的事物上——都能塑造孩子的性格。给他们自信，帮助他们自己做决定，帮助他们身体茁壮成长的同时，心理上也健康成长。帮助他们摆脱总是要听别人告诉自己做什么，不做什么的受挫感。

我们不仅觉得这个很有意义，而且还想把这个消息也传达给你。

我们并不是说，所有从家乐氏谷物套装中，学会自主做决定的孩子，都能自动成长为董事会主席！但是，在给孩子提供早餐的同时，也给他上了人生中的一个小课程，这不是很好吗？甚至是你的丈夫，可能也会觉得自己做决定吃到的早餐格外香甜呢！
