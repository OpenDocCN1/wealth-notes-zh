# 53 Cookbooks later
I switched to Instant Tea

By Ida Bailey Allen,
Author of "Gastronomique"

I GUESS IT'S ONLY human when you've just finished your fifty-third cookbook to think you know almost all there is to know about food.
That's why I was so surprised this summer to learn something really new—and from a young bride, too. She served me the best iced tea I ever tasted. Then she amazed me by saying it was instant tea.
"Oh, come now," I replied. "I tried them all when they first came out—instant tea just can't taste this good."
Well, I was wrong. It seems there's a new kind of instant tea. It's called Instant Tender Leaf and

it's the only one that's 100% pure tea. There are no fillers or carbohydrates to dilute the flavor, so it tastes just the way tea should.
And easy? All my young friend did was dissolve a spoonful of the pure tea crystals in a little boiling water, stir, then fill up the glass with ice and tap water. There's no brewing to wait for and no drippy tea bags to get rid of.
Ever since cookbook number one, it's been a habit of mine to break up long writing hours with time out for tea. And from now on it's going to be Instant Tender Leaf for delicious iced tea with no fuss.
