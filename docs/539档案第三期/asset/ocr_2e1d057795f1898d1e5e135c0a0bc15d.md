# What Americans taught me about iced tea

by Gracie Fields

![](bbox=[0.24, 0.51, 0.31, 0.62])

"Look here, Love," I said to my hostess. "I know you Americans have a national passion for doing things fast. But aren't you carrying it a bit too far?"

"I took it in stride when your husband snapped my picture . . . and showed it to me seconds later. I kept calm when you poured some powder from a packet and turned it into chocolate pudding on the spot.

"But now you say you're going to make instant iced tea. And for a real British tea drinker like me! I'll hold out for good old-fashioned tea leaves, thank you."

"Good old-fashioned nuisance," said my friend firmly. And before I could say "orange pekoe," she hustled me to the kitchen and put a spoonful of Instant Tender Leaf in a tall glass.

"Now watch," she crowed. And, Dear, she turned on the cold water tap. Lots of ice and that was it! She waited while I sipped.

"Well, you could have knocked me over with an ice cube! "It tastes like real tea," I told her.

"It's real tea, silly. Instant Tender Leaf Brand is 100% pure tea . . . none of those carbohydrate fillers to dilute the flavor."

Dear, when we English are wrong we admit it . . . I sailed for home with a jar of Instant Tender Leaf tucked in my suitcase!
