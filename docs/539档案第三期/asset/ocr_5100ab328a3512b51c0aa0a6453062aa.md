# Doctors of Two Nations
Agree as to the Benefits of Beer

American Doctor: To what, Doctor, do you attribute the average of the German people?
German Doctor: To one thing, my dear Doctor, just to their temperance.
American: That Doctor, we think of your people as heavy drinkers.
German: Ah, but the drink is beer. While other intoxicating liquors have their waters, whiskies and stout containing large percentages of alcohol and very little real value, we drink to our beer with its nourishing barley and malted hops and only 3½% alcohol.
American: You say only 3½% alcohol as though that ingredient were not intoxicated.
German: I do not mean it in that sense. We had alcohol has a first and stimulating value when the proportion is not too great. The danger is in overconsumption, injurious when the percentage is so small as in beer.
American: Perhaps the superiority of your people may be due to the superiority of your beers.
German: Don’t mistake them. We are strong admirers of your Schlitz Beer. It possesses the same care as in its brewing. Its full rich taste brings to you the taste of the barley and the hops, so often lost in the different processes. It has the sparkle and life too, due to a perfect yeast. The freedom from germs allows careful preservation. The fact that it is that same deliciousness proves its perfect flavoring, as strong as you say.
