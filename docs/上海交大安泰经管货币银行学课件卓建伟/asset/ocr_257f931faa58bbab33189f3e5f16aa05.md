# Summary Table 1 Response of the Quantity of an Asset Demanded to Changes in Wealth, Expected Returns, Risk, and Liquidity

| Variable | Change in Variable | Change in Quantity Demanded |
|---|---|---|
| Wealth | ↑ | ↑ |
| Expected return relative to other assets | ↑ | ↑ |
| Risk relative to other assets | ↑ | ↓ |
| Liquidity relative to other assets | ↑ | ↑ |

*Note: Only increases in the variables are shown. The effect of decreases in the variables on the change in quantity demanded would be the opposite of those indicated in the rightmost column.*
